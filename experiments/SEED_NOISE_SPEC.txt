THE NOISE FLOOR -- HOW MUCH DOES THE RESULT MOVE WHEN ONLY THE SEEDS CHANGE?
================================================================================
Written 2026-09-02, BEFORE any code is written and before any result is seen.

WHY THIS EXISTS
    diagnostics/purge_fix_measure.txt reported that a 0.01% change in training
    rows (41,380,868 -> 41,376,648) moved INDIVIDUAL YEARS by up to 21 points --
    mid v1 2021 by -21.43, mid v1 2020 by +17.67. The headline shipping-arm
    differences were +0.54 on n100 and -0.81 on mid.

    THOSE WERE JUDGED AGAINST A 0.5-POINT NOISE FLOOR THAT WAS NEVER MEASURED.
    It was invented in PURGE_FIX_SPEC.txt and pre-registered, which makes it
    honest but does not make it correct. This spec measures the floor instead.

    THE PURGE RESULT IS PROVISIONAL UNTIL THIS RUN COMPLETES. If +0.54 and -0.81
    sit inside the seed-change spread, the purge measurement is INCONCLUSIVE AT
    THAT DESIGN and will be reported as such, without softening.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT IS HELD FIXED, AND WHAT VARIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    VARIES:  the model seeds. Nothing else.

    HELD FIXED, bit for bit: the raw feature panel and all 17 features, the
    label, y_rank, the CURRENT production purge (32 calendar days -- deliberately
    NOT the corrected one, so that the production result is itself one draw from
    the distribution being measured), the window 2019-01-01..2026-05-29, both
    universes, TOP_N=8, BUFFER=16, VOL_WIN=60, the 0.98 factor, slippage, the
    Zerodha cost model, CASH_YIELD=0.0, close-signal/next-open-fill, and the
    shipping engine test_exposure.backtest_exposure.

    WHY THE CURRENT PURGE AND NOT THE CORRECTED ONE. The quantity being measured
    is the spread the purge delta must be judged against, and that delta was
    computed on the current-purge baseline. Measuring the spread on the same
    baseline makes the comparison like for like, and it makes the recorded
    production numbers one observation of the distribution rather than an
    outside reference point.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DESIGN -- 40 SEEDS FITTED ONCE, SUB-ENSEMBLES FORMED AFTERWARDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The obvious design is to run K separate 10-seed sets and take the spread of K
headline numbers. THAT IS NOT THE DESIGN CHOSEN, for one reason: it costs the
same and answers less.

    FIT 40 DISTINCT SEEDS, one pass, storing EVERY SEED'S SCORES SEPARATELY
    rather than only the ensemble mean.

    The first 10 are the production set [7,42,99,1,2,3,11,22,33,101], so the
    shipped result is reproducible from the store and serves as an identity gate.
    The remaining 30 are new and are listed in the output.

    Sub-ensembles of any size K <= 40 are then formed by averaging K stored
    score columns and running the backtest on the result. A backtest costs about
    0.1 seconds, so hundreds of sub-ensembles are affordable once the fits exist.

WHAT THIS BUYS
    An EMPIRICAL sigma(K) curve, measured, rather than an assumed 1/sqrt(K)
    scaling. Question 2 -- how many seeds are needed for stability to within 0.5
    points -- is then answered from the observed curve instead of from an
    extrapolation whose exponent nobody checked.

SUB-ENSEMBLE SAMPLING
    K in {1, 2, 3, 5, 10, 20, 40}. For each K below 40, M = 50 random subsets
    drawn without replacement from the 40 seeds. K = 40 has exactly one subset.

    THE SUBSETS OVERLAP AND ARE THEREFORE NOT INDEPENDENT. At K = 10 there are
    C(40,10) possible subsets so the overlap is mild, but at K = 20 and above it
    is substantial and the measured spread is biased DOWNWARD. This is stated in
    advance and repeated beside the K = 20 and K = 40 rows in the report. The
    K = 10 figure is the one the conclusions rest on, because 10 is what ships.

ARMS: v1 and v2 -- the two gated arms, both universes. v3 and v4 are not run for
sub-ensembles; the pro-vol arms are not production and V34_SPEC declined to
promote them.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT IS REPORTED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 1. HEADLINE SPREAD AT K = 10, per arm per universe: min, p5, median, p95, max,
    standard deviation, and the full range, for CAGR, Sharpe and MaxDD.

 2. THE VERDICT ON THE PURGE RESULT. Where do +0.54 (n100 v2) and -0.81 (mid v2)
    fall inside the K = 10 CAGR distribution? Reported as a percentile of the
    seed-change spread. If they fall inside it, the purge measurement is
    INCONCLUSIVE AT THIS DESIGN and the report says exactly that.

 3. YEAR-LEVEL SPREAD ACROSS SEED SETS, per year per arm per universe: the range
    and standard deviation of annual return across the K = 10 sub-ensembles.

    THE SPECIFIC QUESTION: the purge run moved individual years by up to 21
    points. If changing only the seeds moves years by a comparable amount, then
    the purge run's year-level swings were never evidence about the purge, and
    BOTH OBSERVATIONS ARE ONE FINDING ABOUT MODEL STABILITY, not two findings.
    The report states it as one finding if that is what the numbers show.

 4. SEEDS REQUIRED FOR +/-0.5 POINT STABILITY, from the measured sigma(K) curve.
    Reported as the smallest K whose observed spread meets the bar, and -- if no
    tested K meets it -- as an extrapolation with the fitted exponent shown and
    labelled as an extrapolation.

 5. THE sigma(K) CURVE ITSELF, all K, so the shape is visible rather than
    summarised.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A LIMIT THAT MUST BE STATED BEFORE THE NUMBERS ARRIVE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A SEED CHANGE AND A PURGE CHANGE ARE DIFFERENT PERTURBATIONS. Changing seeds
holds the data fixed and redraws the model. Changing the purge holds the seeds
fixed and moves the data boundary by 0.01%. The seed spread is therefore NOT the
exact null distribution for a purge change; it is the best available proxy for
"how much does this result move when something that should not matter changes".

    THE HONEST CLAIM THIS SUPPORTS: if the purge delta sits inside the seed
    spread, the purge measurement CANNOT BE DISTINGUISHED from ordinary re-fit
    variation at this design.

    THE CLAIM IT DOES NOT SUPPORT: that the purge change had no effect. An effect
    smaller than the measurement's resolution is not a measured absence.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WRITTEN PREDICTION -- RECORDED BEFORE THE RUN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 1. THE K = 10 CAGR SPREAD IS LARGE ENOUGH TO SWALLOW THE PURGE RESULT. I expect
    a standard deviation of roughly 1 to 2 CAGR points on v2 and more on v1, and
    a full range across 50 sub-ensembles of 3 to 6 points. Confidence: high, and
    it follows directly from the purge run's year-level swings.

 2. THEREFORE +0.54 AND -0.81 WILL SIT WELL INSIDE THE SPREAD, and the purge
    measurement will be reported as INCONCLUSIVE AT THIS DESIGN. I am recording
    this before the run because it retracts a number I reported four hours ago,
    and I would rather be on record predicting my own result was noise than
    discover it afterwards.

 3. YEAR-LEVEL SEED SPREAD WILL BE DOUBLE DIGITS in the high-return years,
    comparable to the purge run's 21-point swing. If so, the two observations are
    ONE finding about stability. Confidence: high.

 4. SEEDS NEEDED FOR +/-0.5 POINTS: FAR MORE THAN 10. If sigma(10) is about 1.5
    and the scaling is near 1/sqrt(K), the requirement is near 90. My stated
    prediction is that the answer lands between 50 and 250, and that NO TESTED K
    up to 40 meets the bar, so the answer will be an extrapolation.

 5. v1 WILL BE NOISIER THAN v2 IN ABSOLUTE POINTS, because v1 is 100% invested
    and v2 deploys about 55%, which damps everything including noise.

 6. I DO NOT EXPECT THIS TO CHANGE THE PUBLISHED HEADLINE NUMBERS. The production
    seed set is one legitimate draw and its result stands as what that
    configuration produced. What changes is the CONFIDENCE that can be attached
    to any difference smaller than the spread -- including differences this
    project has already acted on.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THIS RUN CANNOT DO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 1. It cannot say the strategy is wrong. A wide seed spread is a statement about
    resolution, not about the mean.
 2. It cannot retroactively re-judge every past experiment. It does establish
    that any past decision resting on a difference smaller than the measured
    spread was resting on less than it appeared to. Which past decisions those
    are is a separate task and is NOT undertaken here.
 3. It does not address leakage, survivorship, or the trial denominator.
 4. Sub-ensembles overlap, so spreads at K = 20 and 40 are biased downward.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
COST, CACHES, OUTPUTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
COST: 40 seed-fits per month instead of 10, which is 4x the fitting cost of one
pass. The last full pass measured 35 min 17 sec for both universes. The
extrapolation is therefore about 140 minutes and IT IS PROBED FIRST and reported
before the full run starts. Sub-ensemble backtests are about 0.1s each and are
not a material cost.

CACHES: named SEEDNOISE_{universe}. The per-seed score store is written to disk
THE INSTANT the fitting loop ends, before any reshape -- the failure mode that
cost 25 minutes of refits on 2026-09-02 is not repeated. No cache written by any
other script is read.

WAITING: the runner is waited on BY PID, plus a completion sentinel written as
the script's last action. A wait that matches its own command line, as
`pgrep -f` did, is not used.

OUTPUTS
    results_{uni}/metrics/seed_noise_headline.csv    per arm, per sub-ensemble
    results_{uni}/metrics/seed_noise_sigma_k.csv     the sigma(K) curve
    results_{uni}/metrics/seed_noise_yearly.csv      per year spread
    diagnostics/seed_noise.txt                       the report

RECORDED IN experiments/EXPERIMENTS.md as its own entry, cross-referencing the
purge run's stability finding. Nothing committed. Nothing else touched.

English only. No verdict string is hardcoded.
