================================================================================

QUESTION

    On the prices cleaned under experiments/DATA_CLEANING_SPEC.txt, does any arm's
    after-tax edge over the investable taxed buy & hold survive price noise too
    small to see, under both execution profiles?

CELLS

    32 cells are tested: arms v1, v2, v3, v4 (arms/registry.py: v1 none/invvol,
    v2 breadth/invvol, v3 none/provol, v4 breadth/provol) on all eight universes,
    nifty50, midcap50, midcap100, nifty100, midcap150, smallcap250, nifty200,
    nifty500. Every cell gets a verdict. The rule applies to all 32, not only to
    cells whose sigma-0 gap is positive.

    Cadence 20. Window 2019-01-01 to 2026-05-29. Tax on, headline: the in-loop
    capital-gains ledger, the last partial financial year settled on the final
    session, nothing sold at the end. Each arm against the investable taxed buy &
    hold, results/bh_held.held_lots, eq_headline, built on the same perturbed
    panel; it takes no cap and is the same under both profiles.

    Research profile: no participation cap. Tradeable profile: the cap of
    profiles.PROFILES["tradeable"] (1.00 x the prior-20-session median volume),
    volume read from the universe's own farm (volume is not perturbed). THE
    TRADEABLE PROFILE IS UNGATED: nothing replays what the port does under it
    (profiles.UNGATED_NOTICE).

    A SINGLE SUPPORTED CELL ON ITS OWN IS NOT TO BE READ AS AN EDGE. 32 cells are
    tested; some will pass by chance.

ONE DRAW

    1. Each raw constituent file is cleaned with results/ratio_clean.clean on
       exactly the rows engine_core.build_panel cleans (PRICE_COLS, dropna, sorted
       by date, calendar sessions).
    2. Then adj_close' = cleaned adj_close * (1 + eps), eps ~ Normal(0, 0.0001),
       for every (symbol, date): one numpy Generator per noise seed, one draw per
       row of each file in file order, files in sorted order
       (noise_parallel.perturb_cleaned_farm). The perturbation comes after the
       cleaning. Each file carries a clean_exempt column marking the cleaned rows.
    3. The panel is rebuilt with engine_core.build_panel(clean=False): no second
       cleaning; clean_exempt is canonical_price's exemption for those rows. The
       production 10-seed monthly ensemble is refitted, in 5 loky worker processes,
       each fit single-threaded.
    4. On that panel: the four arms under both profiles and the investable buy &
       hold.

    At sigma 0 steps 1 to 3 reproduce the published panel exactly (checked
    2026-09-27 on midcap50 and nifty500: DataFrame equality with build_panel on the
    raw farm).

    sigma 0.01%, n = 10, seeds 101, 202, 303, 404, 505, 606, 707, 808, 909, 1010.

    gap = CAGR% of the arm minus CAGR% of the investable taxed buy & hold, both at
    full precision, (end / start) ** (365.25 / calendar days) - 1. sd is the
    sample standard deviation of the ten gaps (ddof = 1).

BASELINE AND STOP RULES

    Each universe runs one sigma-0 baseline before any of its draws. It must
    reproduce diagnostics/tax_on_all_arms.csv (research, arm_cagr),
    diagnostics/tradeable_tax_all_arms.csv (tradeable, tradeable_cagr) and the
    investable buy & hold (bh_cagr), all the 2026-09-27 cleaned figures, within
    0.0001 points. If it fails, that universe stops and none of its draws count;
    the other universes continue. A universe's draws do not start until its
    baseline has passed.

    A universe's measurement is void if any of its eleven records carries a
    harness hash other than the committed one.

    Every record carries config.panel_code_key() and the farm's source digest. A
    record whose key differs from the current one is stale: it is re-run, never
    reused. Re-running a stage with the same seeds and unchanged code resumes it;
    that is not a second attempt. The measurement's directory is
    noise_runs/cleaned/ and starts empty.

    Order of work: stage 1 (nifty50, midcap50, midcap100, nifty100), then stage 2
    (midcap150, smallcap250, nifty200), then stage 3 (nifty500), two draws at a
    time with five loky workers each (scripts/run_noise_stage.sh N). Within a
    stage, the next job is the first in list order whose baseline condition is
    met. The stages run one after another without a stop unless a baseline fails
    or a rule above is met.

ACCEPT RULE, FIXED NOW (owner, 2026-09-27)

    For each cell and profile, an after-tax edge is supported only if at least 9
    of 10 draws have a gap strictly above zero and the mean gap exceeds one sample
    sd (ddof=1) of the gaps. A cell is supported only if it passes under both
    research and tradeable. Anything else is not supported.

    A verdict needs the universe's passing baseline and all ten draws.

NT_VERIFY LABEL

    Every cell also carries its nt_verify result on the cleaned prices,
    2026-09-27 (diagnostics/nt_verify_all_arms_20260927.txt; the 12 NOT VERIFIED
    cells are noise_parallel.NOT_VERIFIED). A supported cell that is NOT VERIFIED
    is reported as "SUPPORTED, NOT PORT-VERIFIED". The label does not change the
    verdict.

REPORTED

    diagnostics/cleaned_noise.txt: per universe the baseline result, per cell and
    profile the ten gaps, mean, sd, min, max and draws above zero, and per cell the
    verdict with its nt_verify label. diagnostics/cleaned_noise_runs.csv: one row
    per (draw, arm, profile). Nothing else is decided from this measurement.
