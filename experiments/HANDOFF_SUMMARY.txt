## 5. SYSTEM CONFIG
================================================================================

PIPELINE:
  17 features (5 families) -> cross-sectional z-score (clip +/-3)
  -> LightGBM 10-seed ensemble, monthly retrain, EXPANDING window, 32-day purge
  -> 20-day forward cross-sectional RANK label
  -> TOP_N=8 buy / BUFFER=16 hold / rank 17+ sell
  -> breadth exposure (share of stocks with positive 20-day momentum)
  -> inverse-vol sizing (w = 1/vol60, normalised); "provol" and "equal" exist as
     alternative modes and are not used in production
  -> signal at day-t CLOSE, fill at day-(t+1) OPEN, 0.15% slippage
  -> real Zerodha delivery-equity costs (brokerage/STT/stamp/exchange/SEBI/GST/DP)

CONSTANTS:
  HORIZON=20, REBAL=20, TOP_N=8, BUFFER=16, VOL_WIN=60, PURGE=32
  SEEDS=[7,42,99,1,2,3,11,22,33,101]
  START_CAPITAL=10,00,000 | CASH_YIELD=0.0 | SLIPPAGE=0.0015
  The 0.98 safety factor is a bare literal in test_exposure.py, appearing twice,
  with no comment explaining its purpose. Its effect is to hold back 2%.

LIVE UNIVERSES: Nifty 100 (99 names) and MidCap150 (148), both 2019-01-01 to
2026-05-29. RETIRED: 58 and 74, frozen on the old year window.

17 FEATURES:
  MOMENTUM(3): mom_20, mom_120, mom_12_1
  REVERSAL(2): rev_5, rev_1
  VOLATILITY(5): vol_20, vol_ratio, idio_vol_60, beta_60, downside_vol_60
  LIQUIDITY(3): amihud_20, turnover_z, vol_price_div
  TREND QUALITY(4): trend_consistency_20, path_smooth_60, dist_high_252, rsi_14

TWO MECHANICS OFTEN MISUNDERSTOOD:

1. FULL COMPOUNDING. invest_val = port_val * exposure * 0.98, where port_val is
   holdings plus cash. Profits are never set aside.

2. EXISTING HOLDINGS ARE NEVER RESIZED. The engine skips any symbol already held.
   Only NEW positions are sized against the target. A held stock is sold only when
   its rank falls below 16. Actual invested % can therefore drift far from target
   -- one logged example is breadth 3.33% against 79.86% actually invested. This
   is also the direct cause of defect (A) above.

## 9. LESSONS WORTH KEEPING
================================================================================

 1. When a backtest looks too good, look for the bug first. Long-short showed
    2526% CAGR; it was a double-counting bug.
 2. Never change two things at once. The EWMA episode changed weighting and
    horizon together and nothing could be attributed.
 3. Always test on BOTH universes. They disagree often enough that one alone
    would have produced the wrong conclusion -- the pro-vol measurement is the
    latest instance, and the disagreement was the finding.
 4. Sub-period splits are more trustworthy than full-period results.
 5. An output that hardcodes its own conclusion will eventually lie. Every
    printed verdict is computed from the run that prints it.
 6. Do not propose a cause without a number attached. Four hypotheses about one
    reconciliation gap were wrong before measurement caught them.
 7. Write the accept rule before the run, then look at the result.
 8. A trade count that should not depend on a parameter, and does, is a defect
    and not a curiosity. Checking that on the four arms is what found defect (A).
 9. A prediction that becomes UNTESTABLE is a different outcome from a prediction
    that FAILS. Collapsing the two overstates what was learned.
10. Verify that an import is side-effect free before relying on it, rather than
    assuming. Same for whether a diagnostic flag changes the numbers it observes.
11. PRESENTATION RULE (permanent): always write "Name -- descriptive tagline with
    keywords", never a bare project name.

## 10. 30-SECOND PITCH
================================================================================

"The system builds 17 features from daily price and volume, normalises them
cross-sectionally, and ranks stocks every month with a LightGBM 10-seed ensemble
using a purge expressed in trading rows and an expanding walk-forward window. It
buys the top 8, sets total exposure from market breadth, sizes positions by
inverse volatility, and executes close-signal / next-open-fill with real Zerodha
costs.

On Nifty 100 it returns 25.78% CAGR at Sharpe 1.87, against 23.74% and 1.32 for
an equal-weight hold of the same names. On MidCap150, 29.71% at 2.03, against
27.77% and 1.50. Maximum drawdown is roughly half the benchmark's in both, but
see the drawdown caveat below before making anything of that.

Every trading day reconciles -- cash, equity and share counts tie out on 1,836 of
1,836 days -- and the execution layer is ported to NautilusTrader, which verifies
against the research engine at 92 of 92 rebalances. Execution timing is confirmed
against the fill record: 977 of 978 and 961 of 963 fills at the next session's
open, none at any close. Randomised scores reproduce none of the return: 0 of 100
permutations beat the real arm.

Caveats, and they are real.
  - SURVIVORSHIP. The universe is today's index members backfilled, and
    point-in-time membership only reaches 2024-03-28 against a 2019 start. Not
    quantified, and not quantifiable with the data on disk.
  - NO OUT-OF-SAMPLE DATA. Training for the last scored month reaches inside the
    reported window. No part of 2019-2026 was held back from fitting as a whole.
  - THE HEADLINE IS NOT STABLE TO BETTER THAN ABOUT A POINT. Redrawing the ten
    seeds gives a standard deviation of 0.97 on n100 v2 and 1.39 on mid. A one-bit
    float difference moved n100 v2 by 0.66 points.
  - NO NOISE FLOOR EXISTS FOR DRAWDOWN AT ALL. The one measurement on that axis
    found MaxDD indistinguishable from a random-selection null. No drawdown
    comparison in this project should be read as an effect.
  - THE TRIAL DENOMINATOR IS AT LEAST 29 and no result is corrected for it.
  - No capital gains tax, no market impact, synthetic fills.
  - Inverse-vol sizing validates on Nifty 100 and fails three of four tests on
    MidCap150, on a different engine from the one that ships.

To make this a product it needs a less efficient universe or new data, not more
tuning."
