PORTFOLIO DRAWDOWN EXIT -- SPECIFICATION
================================================================================
Written 2026-09-03, BEFORE any code and before any number is seen.

REAL MONEY IS INTENDED TO RUN ON THIS. The spec is therefore written to survive
that rather than to produce a favourable backtest. Where a choice exists it is
named and justified; where a constant is underived it is labelled as such; where
a claim cannot be established with the measurements this project has today, the
spec says so instead of proposing a rule that pretends otherwise.

================================================================================
THE GOVERNING CONSTRAINT -- READ THIS BEFORE THE DESIGN
================================================================================
THE WINDOW CONTAINS ONE SEVERE DRAWDOWN. WHATEVER THESE FIVE THRESHOLDS SHOW,
THEY ARE FIVE READINGS OF ONE EVENT.

2019-01-01 to 2026-05-29 contains exactly one crash of the kind a drawdown exit
exists for: March 2020. Everything else in the window is a shallower or slower
decline. So:

  - A THRESHOLD THAT LOOKS GOOD IS A THRESHOLD THAT HAPPENED TO FIT MARCH 2020.
    Its apparent quality is a statement about one month's shape and speed, not
    about drawdowns.
  - THE NEXT DRAWDOWN WILL NOT HAVE 2020'S SHAPE OR SPEED. 2020 was a near-
    vertical fall with a fast recovery. A slow grinding decline of the same depth
    would trigger at a different time, sell into different liquidity, and re-enter
    on a different path. Nothing here measures that case because the window does
    not contain it.
  - FIVE THRESHOLDS ON ONE CRASH IS ONE OBSERVATION, NOT FIVE. The five results
    are not independent evidence; they are five parameterisations read off the
    same episode, and they will covary because of it.
  - A RULE THAT FIRES ONCE IN SEVEN YEARS IS A RULE TESTED ONCE, whatever its
    summary metrics say. This is why section 6 requires the firing dates and the
    flat periods to be reported per threshold, and why the report must state
    plainly if the activity is a single cluster.

THIS CONSTRAINT IS NOT A CAVEAT ON THE RESULT. IT IS THE CEILING ON WHAT THE
RESULT CAN BE. It is placed here rather than in section 9 so that no reader
reaches the numbers before reaching it.

READ SECTION 8 NEXT. It states that the primary objective of
this rule CANNOT BE JUDGED with any measurement that exists today, and that
governs everything else in this document.


================================================================================
0. A CORRECTION TO THE SURVEY THAT PRECEDED THIS SPEC
================================================================================
The survey of 2026-09-03 stated that a portfolio drawdown rule "needs portfolio
value every day, plus a peak carried across iterations. Neither exists." THE
FIRST HALF WAS WRONG AND IT MATTERS, because it overstates the surface area.

    test_exposure.py:242-245
        mtm = sum(q * prices[s] for s, q in shares.items() if not isnan(...))
        pv  = mtm + cash
        eq.append(pv)

pv IS COMPUTED EVERY DAY, for every date in the loop, OUTSIDE the audit guard,
because eq needs it. What the survey correctly identified as missing is the PEAK:
nothing carries a high-water mark across iterations, and eq is append-only -- the
loop writes to it and never reads it back.

So the daily portfolio value already exists. One scalar is missing.


================================================================================
1. PORTFOLIO-LEVEL. NOT POSITION-LEVEL.
================================================================================
THIS SPEC COVERS A PORTFOLIO-LEVEL RULE ONLY: one trigger on the value of the
whole book, one action on the whole book.

WHY PORTFOLIO-LEVEL IS THE ONE SPECIFIED HERE
  (a) It is the rule that was asked for.
  (b) It needs ONE new scalar of state (the peak). A position-level rule needs a
      new per-position record, because `shares` is `{symbol: quantity}` and
      NOTHING ELSE -- test_exposure.py:142 writes `shares[s] = shares.get(s,0)+q`
      and :102 deletes. THE LOOP DOES NOT KNOW ANY ENTRY PRICE. The price paid
      decrements cash and is then discarded.
  (c) The two rules answer different questions. A portfolio rule asks "is the
      strategy in trouble". A position rule asks "is this name in trouble". They
      can fire in opposite directions on the same day.

A POSITION-LEVEL STOP IS A DIFFERENT RULE AND IS NOT ADDRESSED BY THIS SPEC.
Nothing here tests it, licenses it, or constitutes evidence about it. It would
need its own pre-registration, its own state design (entry price, or a per-name
running peak maintained daily), and its own trial charge. Do not read a result
from this spec as bearing on it.


================================================================================
2. DRAWDOWN IS MEASURED FROM THE RUNNING PEAK OF PORTFOLIO VALUE
================================================================================
THE DEFINITION, FIXED HERE:

    peak_t = max(pv_0 .. pv_t)            running high-water mark, all-time
    dd_t   = pv_t / peak_t - 1            <= 0, in fraction
    TRIGGER when dd_t <= -THRESHOLD

pv_t is test_exposure.py:244's existing daily mark: holdings at that day's CLOSE
plus cash. peak_t is the new state. The peak is ALL-TIME within the backtest
window, not rolling and not reset on re-entry -- see 5.4 for why that choice is
load-bearing rather than incidental.

WHY THE PEAK AND NOT ENTRY
  (a) IT IS WHAT MaxDD MEANS IN THIS PROJECT. engine_core.py:370 computes
      `dd = ((eq - eq.cummax()) / eq.cummax()).min()`. A rule whose trigger uses
      a different definition from the metric it is meant to influence would be
      incoherent, and any comparison between them would be meaningless.
  (b) "FROM ENTRY" IS NOT DEFINED AT PORTFOLIO LEVEL. The portfolio has no entry
      date. It is continuously invested from day 1 with names rotating through
      it. Entry is a position-level concept and belongs to the rule this spec
      does not cover.
  (c) IT IS THE INVESTOR'S EXPERIENCE. A holder's loss is measured against the
      most their account was ever worth, not against a starting balance they
      passed years ago.

CONSEQUENCE, STATED PLAINLY: an all-time peak makes the rule HARDER to trigger
late in a rising equity curve and easier after a long flat period. That is a real
asymmetry and it is accepted deliberately, not overlooked.


================================================================================
3. EVALUATED DAILY, NOT AT REBALANCES
================================================================================
THE RULE IS EVALUATED ON EVERY TRADING DAY.

WHY NOT REBALANCE-ONLY. At REBAL=20 a rebalance-only trigger can lag the breach
by up to nineteen trading days. A drawdown rule that acts three weeks after the
drawdown is not the rule anyone means, and its results would describe the
rebalance clock as much as the drawdown.

WHAT DAILY EVALUATION REQUIRES, AND THIS IS THE HONEST SURFACE AREA

 3.1 ONE NEW SCALAR: the running peak. pv already exists daily (section 0).

 3.2 A SECOND DECISION DAY-TYPE. Today the engine decides at exactly one place,
     test_exposure.py:145, `if i % REBAL == 0 and i < len(dates) - 1`. Every
     order originates there. A daily trigger introduces a decision that can fire
     on ANY day, which the loop has never had.

 3.3 A SELL-ONLY ORDER PATH. Selling is expressible only through
     `pending = (targets, keep)` (test_exposure.py:213), consumed on the next
     day's open at :85-102, where `keep` is the set of names to RETAIN. A full
     exit is therefore expressible as `pending = ({}, set())` -- empty targets,
     empty keep -- which the existing :85-102 block already handles: every held
     name is not in `keep`, so every one is sold at the next open. NO NEW ORDER
     MECHANISM IS REQUIRED. This must be VERIFIED rather than assumed, and 4.3
     says how.

 3.4 A COLLISION RULE. If the trigger fires on a day that is also a rebalance
     day, two writes to `pending` compete. THE PRE-REGISTERED RESOLUTION: THE
     EXIT WINS. The trigger is evaluated first and, if it fires, the rebalance
     branch is skipped for that day. Stated now so it is not decided later with
     results in view.

 3.5 A LATCH. `flat` -- a boolean carried across iterations. While flat, the
     rebalance branch does not run and no buying occurs. Nothing in the loop can
     represent "flat and waiting" today; expo_log is appended only at rebalances,
     so Deployed% would otherwise misdescribe what the book did. WHILE FLAT, THE
     RUN MUST APPEND THE REALISED EXPOSURE (0.0) so Deployed% stays truthful.

 3.6 DOWNSTREAM SURFACE, since the point is that this CHANGES numbers:
     nt_strategy.py:450 mirrors the rebalance rule and nt_verify gates on 92-of-92
     agreement; make_cash_series.py, make_stats_both.py and nt_attribution.py each
     carry their own inline book. Four further implementations would diverge. THIS
     SPEC DOES NOT PROPAGATE THE RULE TO ANY OF THEM. The measurement runs in a
     separate script against a runtime-patched copy of the shipping engine's
     behaviour, exactly as the cadence sweep did, and NO ENGINE FILE IS EDITED.


================================================================================
4. WHERE IT FILLS -- THE LEAKAGE QUESTION, AND IT IS NOT OPTIONAL
================================================================================
THE TRIGGER IS EVALUATED ON DAY t'S CLOSE. THE EXIT FILLS AT DAY t+1'S OPEN.

    pv_t uses `prices = px.loc[dt]`, which is the CLOSE (test_exposure.py:242).
    A strategy standing at day t's close cannot trade at that close. It can place
    an order that fills at the next session's open, which is EXACTLY the
    convention the engine already uses for every other order:
    signal at close -> `pending` -> fill at `opens` the next day (:85-102).

A TRIGGER COMPUTED FROM TODAY'S CLOSE THAT FILLED AT TODAY'S CLOSE WOULD BE
LOOK-AHEAD. It would let the strategy exit at a price it could only know after
the exit was possible. This is the same defect class as the purge leak repaired
on 2026-09-02 and it is the single most likely way this rule could manufacture a
result.

 4.1 THE LAST DAY. If the trigger fires on the final date of the window there is
     no next open. PRE-REGISTERED: the exit is NOT executed and the book is
     marked as it stands. No synthetic fill is invented.

 4.2 THE COST OF THE DELAY IS REAL AND IS NOT A FLAW. The book is still exposed
     overnight and through the opening auction, and the exit fills at whatever
     the open is -- with the same 15 bps slippage and Zerodha charges as any
     other fill. If the gap goes against the strategy, the rule pays for it. THAT
     IS THE POINT: the alternative is not a better rule, it is a fictional one.

 4.3 HOW THIS IS VERIFIED IN THE OUTPUT RATHER THAN ASSERTED. The exit fills are
     tested exactly as diagnostics/checkB_execution_timing.txt tests the normal
     fills, and the check is BLOCKING:

       - Every exit fill is tested against FOUR candidate prices, not one: the
         fill day's OPEN, the fill day's CLOSE, the previous session's CLOSE, and
         the previous session's OPEN. Testing only "does it match the open" cannot
         distinguish a correct fill from one matching everything.
       - GATE: fills matching the fill day's open, at the reconstructed price
         `round(open * (1 + 0.0015), 2)` for buys and `(1 - 0.0015)` for sells,
         must be 100% less the rounding exceptions the existing check already
         documents. FILLS EXECUTING AT ANY CLOSE MUST BE ZERO.
       - GATE: every exit fill date must lie one session AFTER its trigger date on
         the panel's own trading calendar. Not merely later -- exactly one
         session later.
       - The trigger date and the fill date are BOTH recorded per exit event, so
         the gap is auditable rather than inferred.

     IF EITHER GATE FAILS THE RUN STOPS and no result is quoted.


================================================================================
5. RE-ENTRY -- A FIXED, PRE-REGISTERED RULE
================================================================================
NOTHING IN THE LOOP CAN REPRESENT "FLAT AND WAITING" TODAY. The re-entry rule is
therefore invented here, and it is fixed IN ADVANCE so it cannot be tuned once
results are visible.

THE RULE:

    While flat, re-enter at the FIRST SCHEDULED REBALANCE (i % REBAL == 0) at
    which BOTH hold:
        (a) at least RE_ENTRY_WAIT trading days have elapsed since the exit
            FILL date; and
        (b) dd_t > -RE_ENTRY_DD  (the drawdown has recovered above that level)
    On re-entry the normal rebalance logic runs unchanged: the book is rebuilt
    from that day's scores at that day's exposure, and it fills at the next open.

    RE_ENTRY_WAIT  = 20 trading days      UNDERIVED
    RE_ENTRY_DD    = 0.5 x THRESHOLD      UNDERIVED

BOTH CONSTANTS ARE UNDERIVED AND ARE LABELLED AS SUCH. No procedure produced
them. 20 trading days is the existing REBAL, chosen so re-entry cannot occur
faster than the strategy's own decision cycle. 0.5 x THRESHOLD is a round
fraction, chosen so re-entry requires the drawdown to have HALVED rather than
merely ticked up by one day's return -- which would produce an exit-and-re-enter
oscillation that is a property of the rule's construction rather than of the
market. NEITHER IS TUNED, AND NEITHER MAY BE TUNED AFTER RESULTS ARE SEEN. If a
later reader wants different values, that is a new pre-registration with its own
trial charge.

 5.1 RE-ENTRY IS AT A SCHEDULED REBALANCE, NOT ON AN ARBITRARY DAY. Re-entering
     off-cycle would require the buy path to run outside i % REBAL == 0, which is
     a second new decision day-type. The exit needs one; the re-entry does not.

 5.2 THE PEAK IS NOT RESET ON EXIT OR RE-ENTRY. It remains the all-time high of
     pv. Resetting it would let the rule re-arm at a lower bar after every exit
     and would make the trigger path-dependent on its own firing history. This is
     a real design choice and it is stated rather than buried.

 5.3 WHILE FLAT, pv IS ALL CASH and dd_t therefore stops falling. The rule cannot
     trigger again while flat, by construction.

 5.4 CASH EARNS NOTHING WHILE FLAT. CASH_YIELD = 0.0 (test_exposure.py:61) and
     this spec does not change it. A flat book earns 0% and the drag is real and
     charged.


================================================================================
6. EVERY THRESHOLD TO BE TESTED, LISTED BEFORE ANY RUN
================================================================================
ALL RESULTS ARE REPORTED, FAVOURABLE OR NOT. There is no "the interesting ones".

    THRESHOLD in {0.10, 0.125, 0.15, 0.20, 0.25}     -- 5 values
    ARM        v2 only -- inverse-vol, mode="breadth", THE SHIPPING ARM
    UNIVERSES  n100 and mid, both, always
    CADENCE    REBAL = 20, the shipping value, held fixed
    CONTROL    the unmodified v2 arm, which must reproduce v34_comparison.csv
               exactly on CAGR, Sharpe, MaxDD, AnnVol, Trades, TC and
               FinalEquity, read from the CSV. IF THE CONTROL FAILS THE RUN STOPS.

0.15 IS THE VALUE ASKED FOR. THE OTHER FOUR EXIST SO THAT 0.15 CANNOT BE READ AS
A CHOSEN WINNER. A single threshold that looked good would be indistinguishable
from a lucky one; a monotone or ragged response across five is diagnostic in a
way one point never is. Two bracket it below (0.10, 0.125) and two above (0.20,
0.25).

WHY ONLY v2. It is the only arm that ships and the only arm with a measured CAGR
noise floor. v1 has a floor but does not ship; v3 and v4 have NO measured floor
at all and were rejected on their own measurement. Sweeping arms that cannot be
judged would add trials and produce nothing readable.

WHY ONE CADENCE. Crossing cadence with threshold is 25 cells and charges 25
looks for a question nobody asked. REBAL=20 is what ships.

REPORTED PER CELL: CAGR, dCAGR against the control, floor verdict, Sharpe, MaxDD,
AnnVol, trades, TC, TC as % of final equity, final equity.

AND, MANDATORY AND NOT OPTIONAL, THE TIMING OF EVERY FIRING -- because the
governing constraint at the head of this spec cannot be checked without it:

    - the DATE of every trigger and the DATE of every fill, listed in full, not
      summarised;
    - the number of trading days flat for each episode, and the total;
    - days flat as a percentage of the 1,836-day window;
    - the calendar-year distribution of triggers, so clustering is visible as a
      table rather than left to be noticed;
    - the realised return over each flat period -- what the book avoided, or
      missed, while out of the market.

THE REPORT MUST STATE EXPLICITLY WHETHER THE ACTIVITY IS A SINGLE CLUSTER. If
every exit across every threshold falls in one episode, that sentence must appear
in the report in those terms, next to the summary metrics and not below them. A
threshold whose entire evidence is one month must not be presentable as though it
had been tested across the window.

================================================================================
8. THE RULE'S PRIMARY OBJECTIVE CANNOT BE JUDGED TODAY. READ THIS FIRST.
================================================================================
THE POINT OF A DRAWDOWN EXIT IS TO REDUCE DRAWDOWN. THERE IS NO MEASUREMENT IN
THIS PROJECT THAT CAN TELL WHETHER IT DID.

    - NO SEED NOISE FLOOR HAS EVER BEEN MEASURED FOR MaxDD, ON ANY ARM. Entry 29
      measured the floor on CAGR only.
    - The one measurement on the drawdown axis is NEGATIVE. Entry 28's shuffle
      test could not distinguish MaxDD from a random-selection null:
      p = 0.37 and 0.36 on n100, 0.20 and 0.44 on mid. Randomised scores
      reproduced the drawdown profile.

SO A RESULT OF THE FORM "MaxDD IMPROVED BY N POINTS" IS NOT INTERPRETABLE. It
cannot be distinguished from noise, and the only evidence on that axis says the
drawdown profile is a property of the mechanics rather than of selection.

WHAT THIS MEANS FOR THE ACCEPT RULE, STATED PLAINLY:

  8.1 THERE IS NO ACCEPT RULE IN THIS SPEC, AND NOTHING CAN BE PROMOTED BY IT.
      Not "no accept rule yet". An accept rule gating on drawdown improvement
      would rest on a floor that does not exist, and this project has already
      recorded what that costs -- entry 28's purge deltas were judged against an
      INVENTED 0.5-point floor, were reported, and were then retracted by entry
      29 when the floor was measured at 0.97 and 1.39. THAT MISTAKE IS NOT
      REPEATED HERE.

  8.2 WHAT IS GATED IS ONLY CORRECTNESS. Three blocking gates, none of them
      about whether the rule is good:
        G1 the control reproduces the published v2 row exactly;
        G2 the execution-timing check of 4.3 passes -- every exit fills at the
           next open, zero at any close, every fill exactly one session after its
           trigger;
        G3 the daily reconciliation holds -- cash, holdings and equity tie out on
           every one of the 1,836 days, as the shipping arm does.
      A failure of any of these stops the run. Passing them says the measurement
      is sound, NOT that the rule is.

  8.3 WHAT MAY BE SAID ABOUT CAGR, AND ONLY CAGR. v2 has a measured floor --
      0.97 on n100, 1.39 on mid. So the COST of the rule is measurable even
      though its BENEFIT is not: a threshold whose CAGR falls by more than the
      floor on both universes has been shown to cost return. That is a
      one-directional statement and it is the only inferential claim this design
      supports.

  8.4 THE ASYMMETRY IS THE FINDING, AND IT SHOULD BE REPORTED AS SUCH. This
      measurement can establish that a drawdown rule COSTS return; it cannot
      establish that it BUYS anything. Anyone deciding to run it with real money
      is making a judgement that is not supported by measurement in this
      repository, and the spec says so in advance rather than after.

  8.5 WHAT WOULD CHANGE THIS: a measured seed noise floor for MaxDD on v2, by
      the method of entry 29. Cost, from that entry's measured rate: of order 130
      minutes per universe. IT IS NOT PROPOSED HERE and this spec does not depend
      on it. It is named so the reader knows what the missing measurement is and
      what it would cost, rather than being left to infer that none exists.

9. WHAT THIS CANNOT ESTABLISH
================================================================================

 9.1 CAPACITY, AND THIS RULE IS THE WORST CASE FOR THE PROJECT'S FILL MODEL.
     Fills are synthetic against QUOTE_DEPTH = 10,000,000 shares at a FLAT 15 bps
     slippage REGARDLESS OF ORDER SIZE. There is no L2 data anywhere in this
     project, no market impact, no queue position, no partial fills.

     AN EXIT RULE SELLS THE ENTIRE BOOK IN ONE SESSION. That is precisely where
     the flat-slippage assumption fails hardest: a full liquidation is the
     largest order the strategy ever places, it is placed into a falling market
     by construction, and every name goes at once.

     THE PROJECT ALREADY HAS A MEASURED INSTANCE OF THIS FAILING.
     diagnostics/liquidity_participation.txt, on mid: of 985 fills, 22 exceed 10%
     of the symbol's prior-20-day median volume, and the worst is a SELL --
     2021-06-07 AIIL, 29,465 shares at Rs 9.59 against a 20-day median volume of
     1,825 shares = 1,614.52% PARTICIPATION. Sixteen times the day's typical
     volume, in one order, on the sell side. The under-Rs-10 band has a p90 of
     282% and a p99 of 1,609%.
     (That artefact was produced on the 1,842-day window and is pre-purge-fix, so
     the exact fills are superseded; the ORDER OF MAGNITUDE is the point and it
     is not sensitive to either change.)

     A DRAWDOWN EXIT WOULD PLACE MANY SUCH ORDERS SIMULTANEOUSLY. NOTHING IN THIS
     MEASUREMENT, OR ANYWHERE IN THIS REPOSITORY, CAN SAY WHAT THEY WOULD FILL
     AT. Any backtested benefit from a full exit is an upper bound and should be
     assumed unreachable in part.

 9.2 NOTHING ABOUT DRAWDOWN, per section 8.
 9.3 NOTHING ABOUT A POSITION-LEVEL STOP, per section 1.
 9.4 NOTHING ABOUT OTHER CADENCES, ARMS OR UNIVERSES. One arm, one cadence, two
     universes, one window.
 9.5 NOTHING ABOUT SEED STABILITY. One ten-seed panel. Entry 29 established that
     redrawing the seeds moves v2 by sd 0.97 to 1.39 CAGR points on its own, and
     whether a threshold RANKING survives that is untested.
 9.6 NOTHING ABOUT REGIME. The window contains one severe crash (2020) and the
     rule's behaviour will be dominated by it. Five thresholds on one crash is
     one observation of the thing the rule exists for, not five.
 9.7 IT DOES NOT MAKE THE RULE SAFE TO RUN. It is one input to that decision and
     it is silent on the objective the rule was built for.

11. WRITTEN PREDICTION -- FROZEN 2026-09-03, BEFORE ANY CODE EXISTS
================================================================================
Written after the design was settled and BEFORE the measurement script was
created. Nothing below has been informed by any output of this rule.

D1  THE CONTROL REPRODUCES. The unmodified v2 arm matches the published row of
    v34_comparison.csv exactly on CAGR, Sharpe, MaxDD, AnnVol, Trades, TC and
    FinalEquity, both universes.
    CONFIDENCE: near-certain. Same engine, same panel, same constants.

D2  EXECUTION TIMING PASSES. Every exit fill matches the fill day's OPEN; ZERO
    fills match any close; every fill lies exactly one session after its trigger.
    CONFIDENCE: high, because the exit reuses the existing pending path rather
    than a new one. IF THIS FAILS THE RULE IS LEAKING AND NO OTHER NUMBER
    MATTERS.

D3  DAILY RECONCILIATION HOLDS on all 1,836 days at every threshold.
    CONFIDENCE: high.

D4  EVERY THRESHOLD LOWERS CAGR ON BOTH UNIVERSES, and at 0.10 and 0.125 the
    fall exceeds the v2 floor (0.97 n100, 1.39 mid) on both.
    REASONING: the rule can only ever sell after a fall and re-buy after a
    partial recovery, so it realises the fall and forgoes part of the rebound.
    The 20-day re-entry wait guarantees it cannot participate in the first month
    of any recovery, and 2020's recovery was fast.
    CONFIDENCE: moderate-high.

D5  MaxDD IMPROVES AT EVERY THRESHOLD ON BOTH UNIVERSES -- AND THIS PREDICTION
    IS UNTESTABLE BY DESIGN. It is recorded so that a later reader can see it was
    expected, NOT because confirming it would establish anything. There is no
    measured noise floor for MaxDD on any arm and entry 28 could not distinguish
    MaxDD from a random null. IF MaxDD IMPROVES, THAT IS NOT EVIDENCE THE RULE
    WORKS. See section 8.

D6  THE FIRING IS A SINGLE CLUSTER IN MARCH 2020 at thresholds 0.15, 0.20 and
    0.25 on both universes -- one exit, one re-entry, nothing else in 1,836 days.
    At 0.10 and 0.125 there are additional firings, most likely in 2022 and 2025,
    but still in single digits.
    CONFIDENCE: high on the cluster, low on the count at the tighter thresholds.
    THIS IS THE PREDICTION THE GOVERNING CONSTRAINT AT THE HEAD OF THIS SPEC
    RESTS ON. If it holds, every summary metric in this measurement is a
    restatement of one month.

D7  DAYS FLAT IS UNDER 10% OF THE WINDOW at 0.15 and above, on both universes.
    CONFIDENCE: moderate.

D8  THE TWO UNIVERSES AGREE IN SIGN ON THE CAGR CHANGE AT EVERY THRESHOLD.
    CONFIDENCE: low-moderate, and this is A DEPARTURE FROM THIS PROJECT'S
    PATTERN -- lesson 3 records that the universes disagree often, and entries 30
    and 31 found disagreement at most cadences. The reason for predicting
    agreement here is that both universes fell together in March 2020, so a rule
    triggered by a shared macro event should act on both in the same direction.

D9  NO THRESHOLD IMPROVES CAGR BY MORE THAN THE FLOOR ON BOTH UNIVERSES.
    CONFIDENCE: high. Combined with D4, the expected reading is that the rule
    costs return; whether it buys anything is not measurable here.

THE PREDICTION MOST LIKELY TO BE WRONG: D8. Every prior sweep in this project has
found the universes disagreeing somewhere, and predicting agreement is predicting
against the base rate. D6's COUNT at 0.10 and 0.125 is the second most likely to
be wrong; its CLUSTER claim is the one I would defend.

WHAT WOULD FALSIFY THE EXERCISE RATHER THAN PRODUCE A RESULT: D1, D2 or D3
failing. Those are correctness gates, not findings.

12. REVISION 2 -- TIME-ONLY RE-ENTRY. Written 2026-09-03, AFTER revision 1's
result was seen and BEFORE any revision-2 number exists.
================================================================================

WHY THERE IS A REVISION 2
    Revision 1's re-entry condition was UNSATISFIABLE BY CONSTRUCTION. It
    required drawdown to recover above -(0.5 x THRESHOLD) while the book was all
    cash at 0% yield and the peak was never reset, so recovery was impossible and
    the rule terminated the strategy: one exit, ZERO re-entries, 84% of the
    window flat. EXPERIMENTS.md entry 33. The error was in section 5 of this
    spec, not in the implementation.

THE ONE CAUSE BEHIND BOTH PRIOR FAILURES
    REVISIONS 1 AND 2 HAVE ONE ROOT CAUSE AND TWO SYMPTOMS: THE PEAK IS NEVER
    RESET, so after the March 2020 breach the rule is permanently in breach and
    can never clear.
      revision 1  could not re-enter -- drawdown could not recover while the book
                  sat in cash at 0% against a frozen peak. One exit, zero
                  re-entries, 84% flat. Entry 33.
      revision 2  re-entered on a timer while STILL in breach and exited again the
                  next close. 77 exits, six years of oscillation, and WORSE than
                  revision 1 on n100. Entry 34.

WHAT CHANGES, AND ONLY THIS
    ON RE-ENTRY, THE PEAK IS RESET TO THAT DAY'S PORTFOLIO VALUE. The high-water
    mark starts fresh from the value at which the book re-enters, and drawdown is
    measured from the new peak thereafter.

    Everything else is unchanged: exit on breach, trigger on the close, fill at
    the next open, time-based re-entry at the first scheduled rebalance at least
    RE_ENTRY_WAIT trading days after the exit fill, waits {5, 20, 40}, thresholds
    {0.10, 0.125, 0.15, 0.20, 0.25}, arm v2, REBAL=20, both live universes, the
    three blocking correctness gates.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT RESETTING THE PEAK GIVES UP -- STATED BEFORE THE RUN, NOT AFTER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE RULE STOPS MEASURING DRAWDOWN FROM THE STRATEGY'S ALL-TIME HIGH.

    A book that falls THRESHOLD, exits, re-enters at the lower value, and falls
    THRESHOLD again has lost far more than THRESHOLD from its TRUE peak -- and the
    rule never sees a second breach OF THAT SIZE relative to the original high.
    At a 15% threshold, two consecutive resets take the book to 0.85 x 0.85 =
    0.7225 of the original peak, a 27.75% true drawdown, while the rule reports
    two ordinary 15% breaches. THREE resets reach 38.6%. THE RULE'S OWN NOTION OF
    DRAWDOWN DRIFTS DOWNWARD WITH EVERY EXIT.

    THIS IS A REAL LOSS OF PROTECTION AND IT IS ACCEPTED DELIBERATELY. What is
    bought is termination of the degenerate behaviour: a rule that can clear its
    breach and resume. What is sold is the guarantee that the trigger relates to
    the investor's actual peak-to-trough experience. THE RULE AFTER THIS CHANGE
    LIMITS THE LOSS PER EPISODE, NOT THE LOSS OVERALL.

    IT ALSO BREAKS THE COHERENCE ARGUED FOR IN SECTION 2. That section chose the
    all-time peak partly because it matches engine_core.py:370's MaxDD, so the
    trigger and the reported metric meant the same thing. AFTER REVISION 3 THEY DO
    NOT. The reported MaxDD is still all-time; the trigger is not. Any comparison
    between the two must account for that, and none is drawn here.
