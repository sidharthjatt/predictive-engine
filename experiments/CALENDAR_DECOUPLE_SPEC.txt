WHY
    results/make_trading_calendar.py:42 sets

        SOURCE_DIR = config.RAW_DATA_DIR / "nifty50"

    so the NSE trading calendar is derived from the RETIRED 58 universe's raw
    files. engine_core._load_calendar() raises FileNotFoundError without the
    resulting artefact -- FOR EVERY UNIVERSE, n100 and mid included.

    diagnostics/PIPELINE_AUDIT.txt section 8(1), dated 2026-08-30, calls this
    "THE STRONGEST SINGLE ARGUMENT AGAINST THE SPLIT AS PROPOSED" and states it
    "must be resolved in any spec". This is that spec. Recorded in
    KNOWN_ISSUES.md under "STEP 0 couples the live universes to the retired 58
    universe".

    THE FILE SURVIVES A FREEZE; THE ABILITY TO REGENERATE IT DOES NOT. The
    calendar must be rebuilt whenever the price data extends past its current
    coverage, which is exactly when the live universes' window next moves.

WHAT WAS MEASURED FIRST, AND WHY THIS IS NOT A GUESS
    A naive re-point of SOURCE_DIR at n100 was measured on 2026-09-02 and
    FAILED: the n100-derived calendar carried 237 dates the 58-derived one did
    not, 107 of them inside the backtest window, with zero going the other way.
    That measurement is recorded in KNOWN_ISSUES.md. Re-pointing alone is not
    the change proposed here, and this spec exists because it did not work.

    The structure of those extra dates was then measured across 2019-2026, both
    universes. It is stated in PART 2 and it is what licenses the design below.


================================================================================
PART 1 -- WHAT CHANGES
================================================================================

1.1 THE SOURCE BECOMES THE SELECTED UNIVERSE
    make_trading_calendar.py stops hardcoding the 58. It takes the universe as
    an argument and derives the calendar from THAT universe's own constituent
    files. The 58 becomes one selectable source among several rather than the
    only one.

1.2 A COVERAGE FILTER IS ADDED
    The union of dates across a universe's files is not the calendar. A date
    enters the calendar only if it is carried by at least COVERAGE_MIN of the
    files ACTIVE THAT YEAR.

    ACTIVE THAT YEAR is load-bearing and is defined here rather than assumed: a
    file is active in year Y if it has at least one row with a non-null close in
    Y. A name listed in 2025 but with no 2019 data must not drag down the
    coverage fraction of every 2019 date. The denominator is per-year for that
    reason.

    A date is evaluated in the year it falls in. No smoothing, no windowing, no
    carry-over between years.

1.3 WHAT DOES NOT CHANGE
    - The output path and file format of data/nse_trading_calendar.csv.
    - engine_core._load_calendar() and build_panel. No consumer is touched.
    - The retired 58 and 74 path. Their scripts, their window, their pinned
      purge_mode and their published numbers are all untouched. See PART 5.
    - The MIN_DAYS_PER_YEAR = 200 sanity guard, which stays as it is.


================================================================================
PART 2 -- THE THRESHOLD, AND WHY IT IS DEFENSIBLE RATHER THAN TUNED
================================================================================

THE MEASURED BASIS IS AN EMPTY BAND, NOT A FITTED CUT.
    Coverage was measured for every date in 2019-2026, both universes, as
    (files carrying the date) / (files active that year). The result is bimodal
    with nothing in the middle:

        ACROSS 2019-2026, BOTH UNIVERSES
          low group     214 dates    coverage  15.4% .. 47.3%
          high group  3,684 dates    coverage  82.8% .. 100.0%
          EMPTY BAND            47.3% .. 82.8%     WIDTH 35.5 POINTS

    Per year, the narrowest gap in any single year-universe cell is 48.0 points
    (mid 2025: low group tops out at 47.3%, high group starts at 95.3%). The
    widest is 84.3 points (n100 2019: 15.7% against 100.0%). NOT ONE DATE in
    2019-2025 falls between the groups, in either universe -- 14 of 15
    year-universe cells have a literally empty middle.

    THE ONE EXCEPTION, STATED RATHER THAN SMOOTHED OVER: 2026-06-08 sits at
    82.8% in n100 (82 of 99 files) and 99.3% in mid. It is the last date in the
    data, it is OUTSIDE the backtest window, and it is the only date anywhere in
    2019-2026 that lands between 47.3% and 95%. At any threshold from 50% to
    82% it is kept in both universes; above 82.8% the two universes would
    disagree about it. See PART 6.

COVERAGE_MIN = 0.50, AND IT IS AN UNDERIVED CONSTANT.
    IT IS NAMED HERE AS UNDERIVED BECAUSE THAT IS WHAT IT IS. No procedure
    produced 0.50. It was not optimised, and no search over thresholds was run.
    It is a round number chosen to sit inside a 35.5-point empty band, roughly
    in its middle.

    WHAT IS DEFENSIBLE IS NOT THE VALUE BUT THE INSENSITIVITY. Every threshold
    in (0.473, 0.828] partitions the 2019-2026 data IDENTICALLY -- a range 35
    points wide. 0.50 is not better than 0.60 or 0.75; it is one of a continuum
    of values that all give the same answer on the measured data. Had the answer
    depended on the third decimal place, this design would be fitting and the
    spec would say so.

    THIS IS NOT A CLAIM THAT THE BAND STAYS EMPTY. It is a statement about
    2019-2026 as the files stand on 2026-09-03. A future delivery could put a
    date anywhere in the band, and nothing here prevents that. See PART 6.

    THE CONSTANT GETS ONE DEFINITION, in the script, named, with this spec cited
    beside it. It must not be duplicated -- see KNOWN_ISSUES.md, "VOL_WIN = 60
    has sixteen independent definitions and no central home".

PART 3 -- WRITTEN PREDICTION, RECORDED BEFORE ANY CODE RUNS
================================================================================

THESE ARE PREDICTIONS, NOT RESULTS. They are written here so they can be judged
against the run rather than adjusted after it.

P1  The calendar produced from n100 at COVERAGE_MIN = 0.50 will be IDENTICAL to
    the tracked 58-derived calendar over 2019-01-01 .. 2026-05-29, as a set of
    dates: 1,836 dates, zero either way.

P2  The same, from mid. Both universes give the same calendar over the window.

P3  Every published figure is unchanged. All four arms, both live universes:
    CAGR, Sharpe, MaxDD, trade count and final equity identical to the values in
    results_{n100,mid}/metrics/v34_comparison.csv as they stand today.

P4  The score panels are BYTE-IDENTICAL. build_panel filters by the calendar; if
    the calendar is unchanged over the panel's range, the panel must be
    unchanged, and if the panel is unchanged the scores must be too.

P5  The retired 58 and 74 artefacts do not move, because nothing about their
    path changes.

CONFIDENCE, AND WHERE IT COMES FROM. P1 and P2 are near-certainties rather than
guesses: the 2026-09-03 measurement already established that filtering the union
at 50% reproduces the 58-derived calendar EXACTLY over this window, identical
sets, both universes. What is genuinely being tested is whether the IMPLEMENTATION
does what the measurement did. P3 and P4 follow from P1/P2 only if the calendar
is the sole channel by which this change can reach a number -- which is itself an
assumption, and is exactly what the gate in PART 4 exists to test rather than
assert.

WHAT WOULD FALSIFY THE DESIGN. Any date difference under P1 or P2. Any moved
figure under P3. Any panel hash change under P4. If a headline moves while the
calendar is identical, the assumption that the calendar is the only channel is
wrong, and that is a finding to report rather than to explain away.


================================================================================
PART 4 -- THE VERIFICATION GATE
================================================================================

THE POINT OF THE CHANGE IS THAT NOTHING MOVES, SO THE GATE MUST TEST THE THINGS
THAT COULD MOVE, NOT ONLY THE CALENDAR. A calendar comparison alone would prove
the artefact matches and say nothing about whether the pipeline that consumes it
produces the same numbers.

GATE 1 -- THE CALENDAR ITSELF. Blocking.
    Build to a SCRATCH path from n100 and from mid. Compare each against the
    tracked data/nse_trading_calendar.csv as a SET OF DATES over
    2019-01-01 .. 2026-05-29.
    PASS: zero dates on either side, for both universes.
    Report the count in both directions, never a single "identical" boolean.
    The tracked artefact is SHA256'd before and after and must be unchanged;
    Gate 1 writes nothing to it.

GATE 2 -- THE PANELS. Blocking.
    Rebuild the raw panel for n100 and mid through build_panel against the new
    calendar. Compare against results_{n100,mid}/metrics/raw_panel_*_cache.csv:
    row count, date set, symbol set, and SHA256 of the serialised frame.
    PASS: byte-identical.
    A panel difference means the calendar reached the data even though Gate 1
    passed, and the run stops there.

GATE 3 -- THE HEADLINE ARMS. Blocking.
    Re-run the four arms on both universes and compare against
    results_{n100,mid}/metrics/v34_comparison.csv, READ FROM THE CSV rather than
    typed into the checker. Compare CAGR, Sharpe, MaxDD, Trades and FinalEquity
    on all five rows of each file, including buy & hold.
    PASS: identical to the precision the CSV carries.
    RESCORING IS NOT REQUIRED IF GATE 2 PASSES BYTE-IDENTICAL -- the existing
    score panels are then provably the correct input, and Gate 3 runs on them.
    If Gate 2 shows any difference, Gate 3 requires a full rescore and the cost
    changes; that is a decision point, not something to absorb silently.

GATE 4 -- THE RETIRED PATH. Blocking.
    Assert that the 58 and 74 artefacts are untouched by the run: SHA256 on
    results/metrics/v5_expanding_cache.csv, results74/metrics/v74_expanding_cache.csv
    and both v2FINAL_equity.csv, before and after.
    PASS: unchanged.

REPORTED, NOT GATED
    The calendar's behaviour outside 2019-01-01 .. 2026-05-29, in both
    directions, as counts and as an explicit list of any differing date. It is
    reported rather than gated for the reason given in PART 6.

EVERY GATE FIGURE IS COMPUTED BY THE CHECKER FROM THE ARTEFACTS. No expected
value is typed into the script. A gate that hardcodes its own verdict is the
defect recorded in HANDOFF_SUMMARY.txt lesson 5 and in KNOWN_ISSUES.md under
"STEP 2's leakage checklist performs no checks".

IF ANY BLOCKING GATE FAILS, NOTHING IS COMMITTED AND THE FAILURE IS REPORTED AS
THE RESULT. The prediction in PART 3 is then wrong, which is a finding.


================================================================================
PART 5 -- WHAT THIS DOES NOT ESTABLISH
================================================================================

5.1 IT DOES NOT VALIDATE THE CALENDAR AGAINST ANY EXTERNAL SOURCE.
    No NSE circular, exchange calendar or third-party dataset is consulted, then
    or now. DDQ_Latest/config/trading_calendar.yaml was verified and rejected
    earlier and is not revisited. This change REPRODUCES THE EXISTING CALENDAR BY
    A DIFFERENT ROUTE. If the 58-derived calendar is wrong about a date, the
    filtered calendar is wrong about it in exactly the same way, and this work
    would not detect that. Nothing here establishes which dates are real trading
    days.

5.2 IT DOES NOT ESTABLISH THAT THE LOW-COVERAGE DATES ARE NOT TRADING DAYS.
    Roughly 45 of the 107 in-window low-coverage dates are recognisable as fixed
    national holidays from their dates alone -- 26 January, 1 May, 15 August,
    2 October, 25 December, and a Good Friday that tracks a movable Easter
    correctly across eight years. The remaining ~62 are not identified. The
    filter removes them on COVERAGE, not on any holiday claim.

5.3 IT SAYS NOTHING ABOUT FUTURE DATA DELIVERIES. See PART 6.

5.4 IT DOES NOT TOUCH THE RETIRED 58 AND 74.
    Their path is unchanged, their window stays on the old year cut, their
    purge_mode stays pinned to "calendar", and their published numbers do not
    move. This spec does not implement, imply or prejudge the pipeline split
    proposed in PIPELINE_AUDIT.txt section 9. It removes one obstacle to that
    split and decides nothing else about it.

5.5 IT DOES NOT ADDRESS SURVIVORSHIP, the trial denominator, or any other open
    item. The calendar is orthogonal to all of them.

5.6 IT IS NOT AN IMPROVEMENT TO ANY NUMBER. Nothing gets better. The entire
    claim is that nothing changes, and a change in any figure is a failure of
    this work, not a result of it.


================================================================================
PART 6 -- BEHAVIOUR OUTSIDE THE WINDOW, AND WHAT IS UNTESTED
================================================================================

THE FILTER APPLIES TO THE WHOLE RANGE THE FILES COVER, NOT TO THE BACKTEST
WINDOW. The files run from 2000 to 2026-06-08 (2026-06-22 for the n100 benchmark
directory, which includes the index file). The calendar is built over all of it.
The 50% rule is applied to every year, including the ones before 2019 and the
tail after 2026-05-29.

WHAT WAS ACTUALLY COMPARED, AND WHAT WAS NOT
    The identity result -- filtering at 50% reproduces the 58-derived calendar
    exactly -- was established over 2019-01-01 .. 2026-05-29 ONLY. That is
    1,836 dates out of the roughly 6,574 in the tracked calendar. THE OTHER
    ~4,738 DATES, ALL OF THEM BEFORE 2019, WERE NOT PART OF THE COMPARISON.

    The pre-2019 years were measured for coverage structure only up to a point:
    the year-by-year union and common counts back to 2000 were taken on
    2026-09-02, and they show the same shape, but THE SET COMPARISON AGAINST THE
    58 CALENDAR WAS NOT RUN OUTSIDE THE WINDOW. Whether the filtered calendar
    reproduces the 58's pre-2019 dates is UNTESTED and must not be assumed from
    the in-window result. Gate 4's "reported, not gated" clause exists to make
    that visible when the change runs.

    THIS MATTERS EVEN THOUGH NOTHING IS SCORED BEFORE 2016. build_panel filters
    the WHOLE panel by the calendar, and training reaches back to 2001. A
    pre-2019 calendar difference would change training rows, and by the finding
    already recorded in EXPERIMENTS.md entry 28, a 0.01% change in training rows
    moved n100 v2 CAGR by 0.54 points. Gate 2 and Gate 3 are what would catch
    it; that is why they are blocking and why Gate 1 alone is not sufficient.

THE TAIL PAST 2026-05-29
    Ten dates lie between 2026-05-29 and the end of the data. One of them,
    2026-06-08, is the single date anywhere in 2019-2026 that falls in the
    otherwise empty band -- 82.8% in n100 against 99.3% in mid. At
    COVERAGE_MIN = 0.50 it is kept in both, so the two universes agree, but the
    margin is 32.8 points rather than the usual 35+, and it is the one place
    where the threshold's value is doing visible work. This is stated because a
    reader should know where the design is closest to its edge.

FUTURE DELIVERIES ARE UNTESTED AND THE DESIGN DOES NOT PROTECT AGAINST THEM
    Nothing here predicts what a new data delivery does. A delivery that adds a
    genuine session carried by few files, or a bad file carrying many phantom
    dates, could put a date in the empty band. The threshold would then decide
    an outcome it was never measured to decide.

    THE HONEST STATEMENT IS THAT THE FILTER IS CALIBRATED ON DATA THAT ALREADY
    EXISTS AND IS UNVALIDATED ON DATA THAT DOES NOT. The regeneration case --
    rebuilding the calendar when the data extends -- is the entire reason this
    coupling matters, and it is precisely the case no measurement here covers.
    A re-measurement of the coverage distribution should be a precondition of
    any future regeneration; that is a recommendation, not part of this change,
    and it is not implemented by this spec.

================================================================================
PART 8 -- THE FULL-RANGE MEASUREMENT FALSIFIED THE DESIGN. 2026-09-03.
================================================================================
Appended AFTER the measurement, which was run before any code, at the reviewer's
instruction and on the concern raised in PART 6: that the identity result covered
1,836 of ~6,574 dates and the pre-2019 region had never been compared.

THE CONCERN WAS CORRECT. THE IDENTITY DOES NOT HOLD OUTSIDE THE WINDOW.

    universe   filtered   58 calendar   filtered-only   calendar-only
    n100          6,572         6,574               0               2
    mid           6,552         6,574               0              22

    Every window year 2019-2026 reproduces exactly, as previously measured.
    ALL failures are pre-2019. The difference is ONE-DIRECTIONAL in every case:
    the filter never invents a date, it only drops dates the calendar has.

THE DATES, LISTED IN FULL RATHER THAN CHARACTERISED
    n100 -- 2 dates, in the 58 calendar, absent from the filtered set:
        2003-03-22  Sat    9/65  = 13.85%
        2017-04-04  Tue   29/87  = 33.33%

    mid -- 22 dates, in the 58 calendar, absent from the filtered set:
        2000-01-03  Mon   NOT IN mid's UNION AT ALL -- mid data starts 2000-01-04.
                          This is missing data, not a filtering decision.
        2003-01-01 .. 2003-01-24   EIGHTEEN CONSECUTIVE SESSIONS, each 29/61
                          = 47.54%, i.e. 2.46 points below the threshold.
        2003-03-22  Sat    3/61  =  4.92%
        2017-04-04  Tue   43/115 = 37.39%
        2017-12-02  Sat    5/115 =  4.35%

NO THRESHOLD REPRODUCES THE CALENDAR. THIS IS NOT A TUNING PROBLEM.
    Splitting every date by whether the 58 calendar contains it:

        universe   calendar HAS               calendar LACKS
        n100       6,574 at 13.85% .. 100%    237 at 1.27% .. 24.24%
        mid        6,573 at  4.35% .. 100%    237 at 1.16% .. 47.30%

    A working threshold must exceed the highest-coverage EXCLUDED date and be at
    or below the lowest-coverage INCLUDED date. That requires
        n100:  24.24% < T <= 13.85%      mid:  47.30% < T <= 4.35%
    Both intervals are EMPTY. The groups OVERLAP by 10.40 points on n100 and
    42.95 points on mid. NO VALUE OF COVERAGE_MIN WORKS, in either universe.

    The binding cases are three genuine sessions with very thin coverage --
    2003-03-22, 2017-12-02, 2017-04-04 -- which sit BELOW the top of the
    excluded group. COVERAGE DOES NOT SEPARATE THEM. A different threshold moves
    which dates are wrong; it cannot make none of them wrong.

PART 2'S INSENSITIVITY ARGUMENT WAS MEASURED ON 2019-2026 AND DOES NOT
GENERALISE. IT IS LEFT STANDING AND IS WRONG OUTSIDE THAT RANGE.
    Part 2 states that "every threshold in (0.473, 0.828] partitions the
    2019-2026 data IDENTICALLY -- a range 35 points wide". That sentence is
    still true AS WRITTEN, because it is scoped to 2019-2026. It was used to
    argue the threshold is a choice within a wide gap rather than a fitted
    value, and THAT ARGUMENT DOES NOT SURVIVE the full range.

    Measured band edges per year show the gap narrowing sharply before 2019:

        mid  2003   low tops at 49.2%   kept starts at 68.9%   gap 19.7 pt
        mid  2016   low tops at 18.9%   kept starts at 50.0%   gap 31.1 pt
        n100 2017   low tops at 33.3%   kept starts at 64.4%   gap 31.0 pt
        n100 2016   low tops at  9.5%   kept starts at 59.5%   gap 50.0 pt

    mid 2016's kept side sits at EXACTLY 50.0% -- on the threshold. For
    2019-2026 the kept side never falls below 82.8%. Pre-2019 it repeatedly
    falls into the 50-70% band. THE EMPTY MIDDLE IS A PROPERTY OF THE RECENT
    YEARS, NOT OF THE DATA.

THE PREDICTIONS IN PART 3, JUDGED
    P1  n100 identical over 2019-01-01..2026-05-29 ......... CORRECT
    P2  mid  identical over the same window ................ CORRECT
    P1/P2 EXTENDED TO THE FULL RANGE ....................... FALSE, and the
        extension is what mattered. The predictions were scoped to the window
        because the measurement behind them was; scoping a prediction to the
        region you already measured is not a prediction.
    P3, P4, P5 .... NOT TESTED. No code was written, so no panel, no arm and no
        retired artefact was produced or compared. They are neither confirmed
        nor refuted, and must not be reported as passing.

WHY THE FAILURE MATTERS RATHER THAN BEING ABSORBED
    Two of the 22 mid dates and one of the 2 n100 dates fall in 2017, and the
    2003 block is 18 consecutive sessions. build_panel filters the WHOLE panel
    by the calendar and training reaches back to 2001, so these are TRAINING
    ROWS, not scored rows. EXPERIMENTS.md entry 28 measured that a 0.01% change
    in training rows moved n100 v2 CAGR by 0.54 points. A calendar 22 dates
    short is a larger perturbation than that, in the same place.

    So the change as specified WOULD have moved published numbers, in a project
    whose entire justification for the change was that nothing moves. Gates 2
    and 3 were designed to catch exactly this. They were not needed, because the
    measurement caught it first and no code ran.

WHAT IS NOT ESTABLISHED BY THIS FAILURE
    That the 58-derived calendar is RIGHT about the 24 dates. It is not
    validated against any external source, then or now, and three of them are
    Saturdays. Whether 2003-03-22, 2017-12-02 and 2017-04-04 were genuine
    sessions is UNTESTED. The measurement establishes only that a coverage
    filter CANNOT REPRODUCE the existing calendar, not that the existing
    calendar is correct.

    That no decoupling is possible. It establishes that THIS decoupling, by a
    single global coverage threshold, is not. Other designs were not measured
    and are not ruled out by anything here.

STATUS. BLOCKED. NO CODE WRITTEN. The coupling recorded in KNOWN_ISSUES.md under
"STEP 0 couples the live universes to the retired 58 universe" STAYS OPEN, and
this spec is now part of its record: one candidate design, measured and refuted
before implementation.
