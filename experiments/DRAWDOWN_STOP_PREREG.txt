DRAWDOWN STOP ON THE FULLY INVESTED ARMS (v5, v6) -- PRE-REGISTRATION
================================================================================
Written 2026-10-02, before any fit of the noise band and before v5 or v6 exist
in code. The scope, the band design and the accept rule were ruled by the owner
on 2026-10-02. The seed store (results/seed_band_fit.py, sha256 prefix
e05d6702080959bd, and scripts/run_seed_band_fit.sh) was committed before this
file, in 9677934. No stop-arm figure at any threshold has been computed when this
file is committed.

The design of v5 and v6 (where the stop lives, the arm registry, file names,
edge cases, logs) is the one approved on 2026-10-02 and is not repeated here.
--arm all stays v1 to v4. v5 and v6 are selected by name and write companion
v34_stop_* files. They join "all" only if this measurement accepts them.

RULE

    v5 = v1 plus the stop. v6 = v3 plus the stop.
    Exit when the arm's close equity (holdings plus cash) is 20% or more below its
    running peak; sell everything at the next open through the normal fill,
    slippage, charge and tax path. Stay in cash for at least one rebalance cycle,
    counted from the day the book is flat. Cash earns 0%. Re-enter at the first
    rebalance day after that with breadth of 0.50 or more, computed as v2 computes
    it, by buying the normal v1 or v3 portfolio for that day. The peak resets at
    the re-entry fill.
    The three numbers 20%, one cycle and 0.50 are fixed and are not tuned.

CELLS

    Judged: nifty50, midcap50, midcap100, nifty100, midcap150, smallcap250, each
    under research and tradeable, tax off and tax on, cadence 20. 24 cells.
    Reported only, never judged: nifty200 and nifty500, 8 cells.
    Window 2019-01-01 to 2026-05-29.

COMPARISONS

    v5 against v1 and v6 against v3, on max drawdown, CAGR, Sharpe and Calmar.
    Each stop arm against the equal-weight buy and hold, reported only.

NOISE BAND

    For each judged universe, 20 seeds are fitted once on the published raw panel:
    the 10 production seeds (7, 42, 99, 1, 2, 3, 11, 22, 33, 101, from
    results/build_scores_step.py) followed by 1000 to 1009. None of the
    production seeds is in 1000 to 1009; seed_band_fit.py asserts it. Every
    seed's scores are stored.

    The mean of the 10 production seeds must reproduce the cached score panel bit
    for bit on every scored row, with NaN in the same places, or nothing is stored
    for that universe and the band is not computed. Stage 1 is midcap50 alone; a
    failure there stops the whole band.

    50 distinct subsets of 10 seeds are drawn from the 20, with numpy generator
    seed 20261002. For each subset, cell and metric, d = stop arm minus parent.
    The band is

        2 x sqrt(2) x sd(d), ddof=1.

    THE FACTOR sqrt(2). Ten seeds drawn from a fixed pool of 20 vary less than ten
    independent seeds. Across subsets, the variance of the subset mean is
    (S^2/n)(1 - n/N), where S^2 is the pool's sample variance and E[S^2] is the
    true seed variance. With n = 10 and N = 20 that is half the variance of an
    independent 10-seed mean, so sd(d) is multiplied by sqrt(2).

    Checked by simulation before this file was committed: 20,000 pools of 20 iid
    standard normal seeds, 50 distinct 10-seed subsets per pool, generator seed
    20261002. The mean over pools of sd(subset means)^2 was 0.050051 against a
    true independent 10-seed variance of 0.100000. Times 2 gives 0.100102, an
    error of +0.10% (Monte Carlo standard error 0.27%). Times 19/10, the factor in
    the earlier draft, gives 0.095097, an error of -4.90%.

    The factor is exact for the ensemble score itself, which is a plain average of
    seeds. For CAGR, Sharpe, Calmar and max drawdown it is approximate, because
    those pass through ranking, and max drawdown is the least linear of them. The
    factor is fixed here and is not revisited.

SEALING

    Only the per-cell, per-metric band is written. No per-subset value, mean
    difference or stop-arm metric is written or printed until the band file is
    committed. No stop-arm figure at the 0.20 threshold on the published panel is
    produced before that commit. The collision gate runs at a forced threshold of
    0.01 on one universe, outside the measurement, and reports only that v5
    differs from v1 and v6 from v3, with the event count.

ACCEPT -- separately for v5 and v6, all four required

    The 32-cell thresholds scaled by 24/32 = 0.75, each rounded in the stricter
    direction:
        criterion 1, 24 of 32 x 0.75 = 18.0 -> at least 18 of 24
        criterion 2, 20 of 32 x 0.75 = 15.0 -> at least 15 of 24
        criterion 3,  8 of 32 x 0.75 =  6.0 -> at most 6 of 24
        criterion 4,  5 of 8  x 6/8  = 3.75 -> rounded up, at least 4 of 6

    1. Max drawdown is shallower than the parent's by more than the band in at
       least 18 of 24 cells, and deeper by more than the band in none.
    2. Calmar is higher than the parent's by more than the band in at least 15 of
       24 cells.
    3. CAGR is lower than the parent's by more than the band in at most 6 of 24
       cells.
    4. In at least 4 of the 6 judged universes, the stop arm exits in at least
       two separate episodes of the parent's curve. Episodes are defined on the
       parent's research, tax-off, cadence-20 equity curve: an episode runs from
       an all-time high to the next all-time high and counts if it falls 20% or
       more below that high. Two exits with no new parent all-time high between
       them are in the same episode.
       If criterion 4 fails, the verdict is ONE EPISODE, UNJUDGEABLE, whatever
       criteria 1 to 3 say.

    REJECT if any of 1 to 3 fails. UNJUDGEABLE if 4 fails.

STATED LIMITS

    The band captures model-seed variation only, not price noise, so it may be
    narrower than the real spread. A narrower band makes criteria 1 to 3 easier
    to pass than under the 32-cell design.

    The v1 second episodes in the judged universes are shallow: 20.3% to 22.2%
    deep (smallcap250 20.3%, midcap100 20.6%, 20.8% and 21.1%, midcap150 21.2%,
    nifty100 22.2%). An exit at 20% in such an episode saves little and pays
    costs and tax. Counting only episodes of 22% or deeper, v1 has two or more in
    2 of 6 judged universes and v3 in 5 of 6, so criterion 4 is fragile for v5.

    The 24 cells are not independent: the universes nest, and the four profile
    and tax settings reuse the same trades.

    The window holds one crash.

    Liquidating the whole book in one session is the worst case for the fill
    model.
