FOUR-ARM AFTER-TAX EDGE UNDER PRICE NOISE -- PRE-REGISTRATION
================================================================================
Written 2026-09-26, before any draw of this measurement. The rule and the
statements marked VERBATIM were set by the owner on 2026-09-26 and are copied here
unchanged. The harness (results/noise_parallel.py, sha256 prefix
d9bac5e74ae9ff19) and the stage script (scripts/run_noise_stage.sh) are committed
in the same commit as this file. No draw has been computed when this file is
committed.

QUESTION

    Does any arm's after-tax edge over the investable taxed buy & hold survive
    price noise too small to see, on any universe, under both execution profiles?

CELLS (VERBATIM)

    Cells: arms v1, v2, v3, v4 on all eight universes, 32 cells, tax on, headline
    CAGR, each measured against the investable taxed buy & hold on the same
    perturbed panel. Both the research and the tradeable profile are computed on
    every draw.

    Universes: nifty50, midcap100, midcap50, nifty100, midcap150, smallcap250,
    nifty200, nifty500. Arms as in arms/registry.py (v1 none/invvol, v2
    breadth/invvol, v3 none/provol, v4 breadth/provol). Cadence 20. Window
    2019-01-01 to 2026-05-29. Headline: the in-loop capital-gains ledger, the last
    partial financial year settled on the final session, nothing sold at the end.
    Research profile: no participation cap. Tradeable profile: the cap of
    profiles.PROFILES["tradeable"] (1.00 x prior-20-session median volume), volume
    read from the universe's own farm (volume is not perturbed). The investable
    taxed buy & hold is results/bh_held.held_lots, eq_headline, built on the same
    perturbed panel; it takes no cap and is the same under both profiles.

NOISE (VERBATIM)

    Noise: sigma 0.01%, n=10, seeds 101 to 1010. Each universe also runs one
    sigma-0 baseline that must reproduce the published full-precision figures
    (research: from diagnostics/tax_on_all_arms.csv; tradeable: from the
    2026-09-26 tradeable tax-on grid) within 0.0001 points. If a baseline fails,
    that universe stops and none of its draws count.

    One draw: adj_close' = adj_close * (1 + eps), eps ~ Normal(0, 0.0001), for
    every (symbol, date), by results/price_noise_measure.perturb_farm, unchanged
    (sha256 prefix c0a78e120d6e948b). Seeds 101, 202, 303, 404, 505, 606, 707, 808,
    909, 1010. The raw panel is rebuilt and the production 10-seed monthly ensemble
    refitted from the perturbed farm; the ten fits run in 5 loky worker processes,
    each fit single-threaded, as in the production path.

    The 2026-09-26 tradeable tax-on grid is diagnostics/tradeable_tax_all_arms.csv.
    The baseline compares every arm under both profiles, and the investable buy &
    hold, with those two files (bh_cagr, arm_cagr, tradeable_cagr). A universe's
    noise draws do not start until its baseline has passed.

    gap = CAGR% of the arm minus CAGR% of the investable taxed buy & hold, both at
    full precision with engine_core.metrics' formula,
    (end / start) ** (365.25 / calendar days) - 1. sd is the sample standard
    deviation of the ten gaps (ddof = 1).

ACCEPT RULE, FIXED NOW (VERBATIM)

    Accept rule, fixed now: for each cell and profile, an after-tax edge is
    supported only if at least 9 of 10 draws have a gap strictly above zero and
    the mean gap exceeds one sample sd (ddof=1) of the gaps. A cell is reported as
    supported only if it passes under both research and tradeable. Anything else
    is not supported.

    A verdict needs the baseline and all ten draws of that universe. A universe
    with fewer has no verdict.

THE TWO CELLS ALREADY DECIDED (VERBATIM)

    v2 on midcap150 and nifty500 already has a verdict under AFTER_TAX_PREREG.txt.
    Those verdicts stand and are not decided again. Their v2 and buy & hold
    figures in every draw must match diagnostics/after_tax_noise_runs.csv to full
    precision; any mismatch voids that universe's measurement.

    "Full precision" is equality with that file's recorded values: CAGR rounded
    to 6 decimals, final equity and tax to the paisa. The check covers the
    baseline and all ten seeds. Their gap statistics are reported under both
    profiles, with the earlier verdict (NOT SUPPORTED) in place of a new one.

REPORTING (VERBATIM)

    All 32 cells are reported under both profiles, including every cell that
    fails. The report states that 32 cells were tested, next to any cell that is
    supported.

    Cells whose nt_verify result is NOT VERIFIED are reported with that flag
    beside the verdict.

    The NOT VERIFIED cells, from diagnostics/nt_verify_all_arms_20260926.txt:
    nifty50 v3; midcap50 v1, v3, v4; midcap100 v2, v3; nifty200 v1, v2, v3, v4;
    smallcap250 v1, v3; nifty500 v1, v2, v3, v4.

    Per cell and profile the report states the ten gaps, their mean, sd, min, max,
    the number of draws above zero, and the verdict under the rule above. Records:
    diagnostics/four_arm_noise_runs.csv (one row per draw, arm and profile) and
    diagnostics/four_arm_noise.txt (the report), written by
    `results/noise_parallel.py analyse`. Per-draw result files and logs are kept in
    noise_runs/four_arm/, which is not tracked.

NO SECOND ATTEMPT (VERBATIM)

    No second attempt, no new seeds, no change to the rule, the cells or the
    harness after the first draw is written. Re-running an interrupted draw with
    the same seed and the same committed code recomputes the same draw and is not
    a second attempt. A change to the harness after the first draw voids every
    universe not yet finished.

    Every draw records the harness's sha256 prefix; the analysis voids a universe
    whose baseline and draws do not all carry the committed harness's hash.

STAGES (VERBATIM)

    Stages: stage 1 = nifty50, midcap100, midcap50, nifty100; stage 2 = midcap150,
    smallcap250, nifty200; stage 3 = nifty500. Order within a stage is fixed.

    Within a stage the work list is fixed: for each universe in the order above,
    its baseline and then seeds 101 to 1010. Two draws run at a time; the next to
    start is the first item in the list whose universe's baseline has passed (a
    baseline can always start). A stage is run with

        scripts/run_noise_stage.sh <stage>

    which resumes on a re-run. Estimated wall time from the 2026-09-26 layout
    measurements: stage 1 about 7 hours, stage 2 about 11, stage 3 about 10.
