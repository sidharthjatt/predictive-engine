QUANTIFYING THE PURGE LEAK -- WHAT IS IT WORTH?
================================================================================
Written 2026-09-02, BEFORE any code is written and before any result is seen.

WHY
    diagnostics/LEAKAGE_AUDIT.txt found ONE real leak. The purge is expressed in
    CALENDAR days and the label in TRADING rows, so the margin underflows across
    a holiday cluster. Measured: the label reaches INTO the scored month in 7 of
    126 months and touches its first day in another 21, identically on both
    universes, because the cause is the shared NSE holiday calendar.

    The leak's EXISTENCE is established. Its SIZE is not. Nothing else in this
    project means anything until a number is attached to it.

    THIS TASK MEASURES. It does not fix anything permanently. engine_core.py is
    NOT modified; the corrected purge lives in a separate measurement script.


================================================================================
PART 1 -- THE CORRECT PURGE, DESIGNED BEFORE IT IS IMPLEMENTED
================================================================================

THE DEFECT, RESTATED IN ONE LINE
    engine_core.py:413  cut = first_scored - pd.Timedelta(days=32)   CALENDAR
    engine_core.py:189  fwd_ret = close.shift(-20) / close - 1       TRADING ROWS

    Twenty trading rows span about 28 calendar days normally and more across a
    holiday cluster, so a 32-calendar-day purge leaves roughly four days of
    margin AND THAT MARGIN IS VARIABLE. Where the margin is consumed, the label
    of the newest training rows observes the scored month.

THE CORRECTION -- EXPRESSED ENTIRELY IN TRADING ROWS
    Let `cal` be the panel's own sorted unique trading dates, and let

        i_first = index in cal of the scoring month's first date
        j       = index in cal of a training row's date

    A label at j observes the close at index j + HORIZON. Define

        gap(j) = i_first - (j + HORIZON)          in TRADING ROWS

    The last usable training index is then

        j_max = i_first - HORIZON - EMBARGO
        cut   = cal[j_max]
        tr    = (date <= cut) & y_rank.notna()

    This CANNOT underflow. It is computed on the calendar the panel actually has,
    so a holiday cluster shifts the date but never the row count.

THE EMBARGO IS 2 TRADING ROWS, AND HERE IS WHY
    EMBARGO = 1 is the strict minimum for "does not touch": it puts the last
    observed close at i_first - 1, strictly before the first scored date, which
    already eliminates both the 7 negative-gap and the 21 zero-gap months.

    EMBARGO = 2 IS CHOSEN, FOR ONE STATED REASON. The close at i_first - 1 is the
    BASE PRICE of the scored period's first return -- the strategy signals at the
    close of a day and fills at the next open, so the close immediately preceding
    the scored month is the denominator of the first return that month produces.
    A label that observes i_first - 1 has observed the scored period's starting
    price. EMBARGO = 2 puts the last observed close at i_first - 2, strictly
    before that base price.

    IT IS NOT LARGER, AND THAT IS ALSO A DECISION. A bigger embargo removes more
    training rows, which changes results for a reason that has nothing to do with
    leakage. A measurement that conflated "leak removed" with "trained on less
    data" would not answer the question asked. 2 is the smallest value that
    satisfies the requirement with a reason attached to each of its two rows.

WHAT THE CORRECTION GUARANTEES
    gap(j) >= EMBARGO = 2 for every training row in every scoring month, on both
    universes, BY CONSTRUCTION rather than by luck of the calendar.

    Both failure modes are eliminated: no month at a negative gap, and no month
    at zero. Gap = 0 is treated as contamination, not as a boundary case.

THE CORRECTION IS NOT ALWAYS MORE CONSERVATIVE, AND THIS MATTERS FOR READING THE
RESULT. In months where the old realised gap was 4 (the observed maximum), the
calendar purge was MORE conservative than necessary and the corrected cut sits
LATER, admitting slightly MORE training data. In the 7 failing months it sits
EARLIER. So the training set moves in BOTH directions across the walk, and the
change is not a uniform reduction in sample size.

PART 2 -- WHAT IS MEASURED
================================================================================
RE-RUN THE FULL WALK on both live universes with ONLY the purge changed.

    IDENTICAL: the panel and its 17 features, the label, the window
    2019-01-01..2026-05-29, SEEDS = [7,42,99,1,2,3,11,22,33,101], the monthly
    expanding retrain, TOP_N=8, BUFFER=16, VOL_WIN=60, the 0.98 factor, slippage,
    the Zerodha cost model, CASH_YIELD=0.0, close-signal/next-open-fill, and the
    shipping engine test_exposure.backtest_exposure.

    CHANGED: the purge, and nothing else.

    The feature panel is NOT rebuilt. score_monthly consumes an existing panel
    that already carries the features and y_rank, so re-scoring re-fits the
    models without recomputing a single feature. That keeps the comparison exact
    on everything except the training mask.

REPORTED, PER ARM PER UNIVERSE
    CAGR%, Sharpe, MaxDD%, trade count -- corrected against currently recorded,
    with the difference. All four arms; v1 and v2 are the ones that matter.

REPORTED PER YEAR AS WELL AS HEADLINE
    CAGR by calendar year for the corrected and the current run, per arm per
    universe, with the difference.

    THE SPECIFIC QUESTION: five of the seven leaking months fall inside the
    backtest window -- 2019-09, 2020-05, 2021-05, 2023-05, 2026-05. If the leak
    were doing the work, the damage should concentrate in 2019, 2020, 2021, 2023
    and 2026 and leave 2022, 2024 and 2025 largely alone.

    IF IT IS NOT CONCENTRATED THERE, THAT IS A FINDING IN ITSELF and is reported
    as one rather than being explained away.

PURGE VERIFICATION, RE-RUN
    The Check 2 measurement is re-run against the corrected cut and must show, on
    all 126 months and both universes:
        minimum gap >= EMBARGO,  and no month at 0 or below.
    New min / median / max reported beside the old.

IDENTITY GATE
    The CURRENT-purge arm re-run in this harness must reproduce the recorded v2
    figures -- 25.49 / 1.88 / -18.64 on n100 and 30.22 / 2.06 / -18.98 on mid,
    READ FROM v34_comparison.csv, not typed in. If it does not, the harness is
    wrong and NO difference is reported. Correctness gate, not a performance gate.


================================================================================
A CONFOUND THAT MUST BE STATED BEFORE THE NUMBERS ARRIVE
================================================================================
CHANGING THE PURGE CHANGES THE TRAINING SET IN EVERY MONTH, NOT ONLY THE SEVEN.
The corrected cut lands on a different date from the calendar cut in most months
-- earlier in some, later in others. So every month is re-fit on a slightly
different sample, every score moves a little, and the resulting difference in
returns is NOT a clean estimate of "what the leak was worth".

    IT IS AN ESTIMATE OF "WHAT CORRECTING THE PURGE IS WORTH", which is the
    decision-relevant quantity but is NOT the same thing.

    Isolating the leak alone would need a design that changes the cut ONLY in the
    seven months and leaves the other 119 byte-identical. That is a different
    measurement and it is NOT run here. This limitation is stated in advance so
    it cannot be presented afterwards as a subtlety that was noticed later.

LIGHTGBM SEED NOISE IS NOT SEPARATED EITHER. Ten seeds are averaged, which damps
it, but the same ten seeds fitted on a slightly different sample produce a
slightly different ensemble. No noise floor is established here, so a small
difference cannot be distinguished from re-fit noise.

    CONSEQUENCE, PRE-REGISTERED: a difference under roughly 0.5 CAGR points will
    NOT be called an effect. It will be reported as a number and described as
    indistinguishable from re-fit noise at this design.


================================================================================
WRITTEN PREDICTION -- RECORDED BEFORE THE RUN
================================================================================
Recorded so that whatever happens cannot afterwards be described as expected.

 1. THE CAGR CHANGE IS SMALL. I expect |dCAGR| UNDER 1.5 POINTS on v1 and v2 on
    both universes, and I would not be surprised by under 0.5.

    REASONING, GIVEN SO IT CAN BE JUDGED WRONG: the contamination is in the LABEL
    of the newest training rows only, not in the features of the rows being
    scored. In an expanding window that reaches back to 2016, the affected rows
    are a low single-digit percentage of the training sample, and within those
    rows only 1 or 2 days of a 20-day label are contaminated. The model never
    sees the scored month's outcomes for the names it is ranking.

 2. DIRECTION: I expect the corrected numbers to be LOWER, because removing an
    information advantage should remove return. BUT I HOLD THIS WEAKLY. The
    corrected cut admits MORE training data in the months where the calendar
    purge over-purged, and more data can help. I would put it around 60/40
    toward lower, which is barely better than a coin.

 3. THE DAMAGE WILL NOT BE CONCENTRATED IN THE FIVE AFFECTED YEARS. This is my
    most specific and most falsifiable prediction. Because the corrected cut moves
    in EVERY month, every month is re-fit and every year's return will move. If
    the yearly differences turn out to be concentrated in 2019, 2020, 2021, 2023
    and 2026 while 2022, 2024 and 2025 stay put, MY MODEL OF THIS LEAK IS WRONG
    and that is the more valuable result.

 4. THE PURGE VERIFICATION WILL PASS CLEANLY -- minimum gap exactly 2 on both
    universes, since the construction makes it exact rather than approximate.
    Confidence: high. If it does not, the implementation is wrong, not the design.

 5. I DO NOT EXPECT THIS TO OVERTURN THE HEADLINE RESULTS. If I am wrong and it
    does, that is stated first and plainly, before anything else in the report.


================================================================================
COST
================================================================================
Re-scoring is the expensive step: 126 monthly re-fits x 10 seeds x 2 universes.
A SINGLE-MONTH PROBE IS TIMED FIRST and the extrapolated cost REPORTED BEFORE the
full run starts, not after. The feature panel is not rebuilt, which removes the
slowest part of the normal pipeline.

CACHES: this measurement writes its own, named PURGEFIX_{universe}. It reads the
production raw panel and asserts the universe from the file. No cache written by
any other script is read, and none of its own is read by anything else.
