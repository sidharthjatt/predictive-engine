LEAKAGE AUDIT -- IS THE MODEL SEEING THE FUTURE ANYWHERE?
================================================================================
Written 2026-09-01, BEFORE any check is written and before any result is seen.

WHY THIS EXISTS, AND WHY THE SHUFFLE TEST DOES NOT ANSWER IT
    EXPERIMENTS.md entry 28 passed at p = 0.0099 with 0 of 100 permutations
    beating the real arm. THAT RESULT IS CONSISTENT WITH REAL EDGE AND WITH
    LEAKAGE, AND CANNOT SEPARATE THEM.

    A feature that looks into the future produces genuinely good scores. The
    shuffle destroys the symbol-to-score mapping, so it detects whether the
    scores carry information. It has no way to ask WHERE that information came
    from. A leaking model passes the shuffle test loudly -- more loudly than an
    honest one.

    So a strong shuffle result raises the value of this audit rather than
    lowering it.

MEASURE, DO NOT REASON. Every statement in the report must come from a
computation on the panel. Where a check cannot be run with what is on disk, the
report says so plainly and says what is missing, rather than substituting an
argument.

NOTHING IS FIXED. This audit REPORTS. Any repair is a separate decision with its
own spec. A found leak is worth more than a clean report.


================================================================================
CHECK 1 -- FEATURE CAUSALITY, MEASURED
================================================================================
THE TEST
    For each of the 17 features in FEATS_V2, and for a sample of dates t across
    the window: compute the feature at day t on the real panel; then CORRUPT every
    row strictly after t and recompute the feature at day t; compare.

    BIT-EXACT COMPARISON. Not "close", not a tolerance. A causal feature cannot
    move at all when only future rows change.

CORRUPTION
    Two independent corruptions, because one could coincidentally leave a value
    unchanged:
      (a) REPLACE   rows after t get prices drawn from noise
      (b) SHUFFLE   rows after t are permuted in time
    A feature must be bit-exact under BOTH to pass.

TWO LEVELS, BECAUSE FEATURES ARE BUILT AT TWO LEVELS
    add_stock_features runs PER SYMBOL. add_market_relative_features runs at PANEL
    level and is where beta_60 and idio_vol_60 obtain a market return. A
    per-symbol-only test would not see a leak entering through the market series,
    so the corruption is applied to the WHOLE PANEL and both stages are recomputed.

SAMPLING
    Dates spread across the window rather than clustered, and at least 20 of them,
    so that a feature which leaks only near a boundary is not missed by a lucky
    sample. Symbols: enough that a per-symbol quirk does not carry the result.
    Exact counts are reported with the result.

REPORTED
    THE LIST, PER FEATURE, NOT A SUMMARY VERDICT. For each of the 17: bit-exact
    under (a), bit-exact under (b), and if not, the magnitude and the dates where
    it moved. A single feature failing is a finding and is named.

WRITTEN PREDICTION
    I expect most of the 17 to be bit-exact. I have NOT read the feature
    implementations at the time of writing this, deliberately, so the prediction
    is a real one.

    The three I consider most at risk, from their NAMES alone:
      turnover_z       -- a "z" suffix implies a standardisation. If its mean and
                          standard deviation are taken over a symbol's WHOLE
                          history rather than a trailing window, the day-t value
                          embeds future data. THIS IS MY SINGLE MOST LIKELY
                          FAILURE.
      dist_high_252    -- distance from a 252-day high. Correct if rolling(252),
                          leaking if expanding or whole-sample.
      vol_ratio        -- a ratio of two volatility measures; leaks if either
                          side is normalised over the full sample.

    Stated probability that at least one of the 17 fails: I put it near even. I
    would not be surprised by 17 of 17 clean and I would not be surprised by one
    failure.


================================================================================
CHECK 2 -- THE TRAINING MASK AND WHETHER THE PURGE PURGES
================================================================================
WHAT THE CODE DOES, READ FROM SOURCE BEFORE THE CHECK WAS DESIGNED
    engine_core.score_monthly:413-415

        cut = p.loc[p.ym == ym, "date"].min() - pd.Timedelta(days=purge)
        tr  = (p["date"] <= cut) & p["y_rank"].notna()

    and the label at engine_core:189

        d["fwd_ret"] = d["close"].shift(-horizon) / d["close"] - 1

    THE UNITS DO NOT MATCH, AND THAT IS THE WHOLE POINT OF THIS CHECK.
    PURGE = 32 is CALENDAR days (pd.Timedelta(days=32)). HORIZON = 20 is TRADING
    ROWS (shift(-20)). Twenty trading rows span about 28 calendar days in a normal
    month and MORE across a holiday cluster. The margin is therefore small and
    VARIABLE, and whether it is ever negative is an empirical question.

THE TEST
    For every scoring month in the walk:
      1. Dump the actual set of dates present in the training sample.
      2. Assert no training row is dated on or after the scoring month's first
         date. Report any violation with the dates.
      3. For the LATEST training row, compute where its label actually reaches --
         the date of the row HORIZON trading rows later, per symbol, on the real
         calendar. This is the date the label observes.
      4. GAP = (scoring month's first date) minus (the date the label observes),
         in trading days and in calendar days.

    THE MINIMUM GAP ACROSS ALL SCORING MONTHS IS THE ANSWER.

        gap > 0    the purge holds: no label observed data from the scored month
        gap = 0    the label observes the first scored day
        gap < 0    THE LABEL REACHES INTO THE SCORED MONTH. That is leakage, and
                   its size is the number of days by which it overlaps.

    Reported per month, not only as a minimum, so a single bad month is visible.

REPORTED ALSO
    The number of training rows per month, the latest training date per month, and
    the distribution of gaps. The purge's NOMINAL value (32 calendar days) beside
    its REALISED effect in trading days.

WRITTEN PREDICTION
    The gap is POSITIVE BUT SMALL -- I expect a median of roughly 2 to 5 trading
    days. I expect the MINIMUM to be at or near zero, and I think there is a
    material chance it is NEGATIVE in at least one month, because a 32-calendar-day
    purge against a 20-trading-row label leaves about 4 calendar days of margin in
    a clean month and a holiday cluster can consume that.

    IF THE MINIMUM IS NEGATIVE, THAT IS A REAL LEAK, it is small and bounded, and
    it is reported as a leak without softening.


================================================================================
CHECK 3 -- NORMALISATION SCOPE
================================================================================
THE TEST
    Enumerate EVERY scaling, ranking, percentile or standardisation applied to a
    feature or to the label, anywhere between the raw CSV and the model's input.
    For each, state from the code:

        WHERE     file and line
        WHAT      the operation
        SCOPE     per-day cross-sectional / per-training-window / whole-panel
        VERDICT   whole-panel scope on anything the model sees is a LEAK

    Per-day cross-sectional is safe: it uses only same-day peers, which is
    contemporaneous information, not future information. Per-training-window is
    safe. WHOLE-PANEL IS NOT, because the statistic is fitted using rows the model
    should not have seen.

    Found by grep over the feature and panel modules for the operations
    themselves -- mean, std, rank, quantile, transform, scale, normalize,
    expanding, cumulative -- rather than by reading for intent.

RELATIONSHIP TO CHECK 1
    Check 1 would CATCH a whole-panel normalisation automatically, because
    corrupting future rows would move the day-t value. Check 3 is run anyway and
    independently: it locates the code, and it catches a scope error in a place
    Check 1 does not sample. Agreement between the two is itself information; a
    disagreement is a finding about the audit and is reported as one.

WRITTEN PREDICTION
    cross_sectional_normalize groups by date (features_v2.py:194-206) and y_rank
    groups by date (engine_core.py:277-279), so BOTH are per-day and safe. My
    prediction is that the panel-level normalisation is clean and that if a
    whole-panel statistic exists it is INSIDE a feature, in add_stock_features --
    which is the same suspicion as turnover_z in Check 1.

CHECK 4 -- CORPORATE ACTIONS
================================================================================
STATED IN ADVANCE, FROM SOURCE
    engine_core.build_panel:180 selects

        raw[["date", "open", "high", "low", "close", "volume"]]

    The raw CSVs carry BOTH close AND adj_close. THE PANEL USES close AND IGNORES
    adj_close. Whether close is itself adjusted is NOT stated anywhere and is the
    first thing this check measures.

THE TEST
    1. Measure how often close and adj_close differ, per universe: number of rows,
       number of symbols, and the size of the differences. If they never differ,
       one of them is redundant; if they differ often, the panel is built on the
       column that is NOT the adjusted one.
    2. Find the top 20 largest single-day returns, positive and negative, per
       universe, on the panel as the engine builds it.
    3. For each, report the date, symbol, magnitude, whether the EXTREME_RET guard
       masked it, and whether adj_close shows the same move.

       THE ADJUSTED COLUMN IS THE INSTRUMENT. If close falls 50% on a day when
       adj_close does not, that is an unadjusted corporate action in the column
       the engine trades on. That is a MEASUREMENT, not an inference from the
       round number.

WHAT IS MISSING, STATED NOW RATHER THAN AS AN EXCUSE LATER
    THERE IS NO CORPORATE-ACTION DATASET ON DISK. data/reference/ holds NSE
    circulars, a press-release manifest and the symbol-rename map, and nothing
    else. So "check them against known corporate actions" CANNOT be done against
    an authoritative split and bonus list. It can be done against adj_close, which
    is a proxy of unknown provenance.

    THE REPORT WILL SAY WHICH CLAIMS REST ON adj_close AND THAT ITS PROVENANCE IS
    UNVERIFIED. Confirming any individual split against NSE would be a manual
    lookup per name and is out of scope here; the report will name the candidates
    so that lookup is possible.

THE GUARD, AND ITS GAP
    features_v2.py sets EXTREME_RET_HI = 0.55 and EXTREME_RET_LO = -0.35 and NaNs
    returns outside that band. Its own comment states that close-derived features
    still span the level shift on surviving rows.

    ARITHMETIC CONSEQUENCE, TO BE CONFIRMED BY MEASUREMENT: a 2-for-1 split is a
    -50% move and IS caught. A 5-for-4 bonus is -20% and IS NOT. So the guard
    catches large splits and misses small ones by construction. The check measures
    how many extreme moves fall in the UNCAUGHT band, -35% to -20%.

WRITTEN PREDICTION
    1. close and adj_close DIFFER on a material number of rows, and the panel is
       therefore built on unadjusted or differently-adjusted prices.
    2. The top negative single-day returns contain corporate actions rather than
       market moves.
    3. Some fall in the -20% to -35% band that the guard does not catch.
    4. THIS IS NOT LEAKAGE. It is a data-correctness problem and it can push CAGR
       in EITHER direction. It is in this audit because it is the other way a
       backtest produces returns that are not real, and it is reported separately
       from the three leakage checks rather than blended with them.


================================================================================
WHAT THIS AUDIT CANNOT DO
================================================================================
 1. IT CANNOT PROVE THE ABSENCE OF LEAKAGE. It tests four specific mechanisms. A
    clean result means these four are clean, not that the model is honest.
 2. IT DOES NOT ADDRESS SURVIVORSHIP. Separate, unmeasurable today, recorded in
    diagnostics/membership/STATUS.md.
 3. IT DOES NOT ADDRESS THE TRIAL DENOMINATOR.
 4. CHECK 1 SAMPLES DATES. A leak confined to dates outside the sample survives.
 5. CHECK 4 HAS NO AUTHORITATIVE CORPORATE-ACTION SOURCE, as stated above.
