HOW d IS MEASURED

    Metrics are computed from the equity curves with the band writer's
    raw_metrics, unrounded, so d is measured exactly as the band was:
    parent curves from results_<u>/metrics/v34_equity<suffix>.csv, stop curves
    from v34_stop_equity<suffix>.csv. d = stop arm minus parent. MaxDD% is
    negative, so d > 0 means the stop arm's drawdown is shallower. The tables
    below are the output of results/drawdown_stop_judge.py.

    Parent episodes for criterion 4 are found by the script from the parent's
    research, tax-off curve and match the list in the pre-registration exactly.
    An exit is dated by its trigger (the close at which the drawdown reached
    20%); the fill at the next open falls in the same episode in every case.
