PALETTE CEILING -- what a fifth universe's six colours can actually achieve
===============================================================================
Measured 2026-09-19, while costing midcap100. Nothing here is quoted from an
earlier survey; every number below was re-derived on the registry as it stands
at four universes / 24 slots.

METHOD. Grid over the sRGB cube at step 4 (262,144 points), CIEDE2000 in
CIE L*a*b*, colour-blind simulation by Vienot 1999 as implemented in
palette_distance.py. Readable band is L* 30-72 -- the band a line reads on
white, and the band nifty50's palette was drawn from.


1. CO-APPEARANCE: THERE IS NO REDUCTION. THE GLOBAL RULE IS THE REAL RULE.
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every image any producer can draw that reads `chart_colours` was enumerated:

    make_chart.py                 reads NOTHING. No reference to chart_colours
                                  in the file; it hardcodes six colours and
                                  draws every universe in them. Imposes no
                                  constraint. (See the never-wired entry in
                                  KNOWN_ISSUES.md -- this is instance 5.)
    canonical combined chart      any >=2 universes, arms CANON_ARMS = v2+v1
                                  -> slots {0,1} + bh=2 + ix=3
    arm-selection combined chart  make_combined_universes.py:410, any arm
                                  subset other than {v2,v1}, into a SUFFIXED
                                  file -> slots {ARM_SLOT[a]} + {2,3}, and
                                  ARM_SLOT = {v2:0, v1:1, v3:4, v4:5}
    PAIR_CHART                    fixed (nifty100, midcap150), CANON_ARMS
                                  -> slots {0,1,2,3} of those two

    images enumerated                       166
    co-appearing slot pairs                 276
    full cross product of 24 slots          276
    pairs that can NEVER share an image       0

`--arm all` is the DEFAULT and emits a four-arm chart beside the published
two-arm one, so slots 4 and 5 are drawn alongside 0, 1, 2 and 3 for every
selected universe. There is no pair of the 24 slots that cannot land on one
image. THE "AGAINST ALL 24 SLOTS" RULE IS NOT OVER-STRICT; it is exactly the
co-appearance requirement, and constraining to co-appearing pairs only changes
nothing.


2. THE L* HOLE: dE 10 IS UNREACHABLE INSIDE THE READABLE BAND
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Colours clearing dE2000 >= 10 against all 24 slots, all three visions:

    anywhere in sRGB                     1,927 of 40,000 sampled
    of those, inside L* 30-72                0
    L* range of the qualifying pool       1.7 .. 99.0

The pool is near-black and near-white with a hole exactly where a line is
readable. A 6-tuple at dE 10 EXISTS but only outside the band: e.g.
#dcf4f2 #f9ee87 #090a8b #a9facb #140d26 #23edfa, at L* 94.5 93.0 16.2 92.2 5.2
85.9 -- four of them near-white.

On the systematic step-4 grid, exactly FOUR in-band colours clear dE >= 10:

    dE 10.25   #588050   L* 49.6
    dE 10.11   #647c50   L* 49.1
    dE 10.02   #687c54   L* 49.5
    dE 10.02   #5c8050   L* 49.8

All four are the same olive green, mutually closer than 10. The largest
mutually-separated subset is 1. Six are needed.


3. THE BINARY SEARCH: THE LARGEST T FOR WHICH A 6-TUPLE EXISTS IN BAND
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Binary searched to 1e-4, 400 randomised greedy restarts per probe. T is
reported unrounded, because rounding it to 10 or to 8.5 is how a measured
ceiling becomes a remembered one.

  (a) CONSTRAINED UNDER NORMAL VISION ONLY

      T = 20.655653
      #405000 #8c8010 #cca890 #805014 #acacd8 #fc7478
      L*  31.4  53.0  71.5  38.5  71.7  65.4

      worst pair, normal   dE 20.720   midcap100/3 vs midcap50/2
      worst pair, deutan   dE  2.560   midcap100/4 vs midcap150/1
      worst pair, protan   dE  3.349   midcap100/0 vs midcap100/3

      **THIS TUPLE IS REJECTED.** T is high, and it is high because normal
      vision is not the binding constraint and never was. Optimising against it
      alone produces a tuple whose deutan collision, 2.560, is WORSE THAN
      ANYTHING CURRENTLY SHIPPED -- worse than the 3.26 that is already the
      project's worst pair. A threshold measured on the vision that is not
      binding is not a ceiling, it is a number.

  (b) CONSTRAINED ACROSS ALL THREE VISIONS -- TAKEN

      T = 8.712634
      #5c804c #d89094 #a05464 #fc606c #2c503c #9c2000
      L*  49.8  67.0  45.3  61.8  30.9  34.2

      worst pair, normal   dE 10.449   midcap100/5 vs nifty100/0
      worst pair, deutan   dE  8.849   midcap100/2 vs midcap150/4
      worst pair, protan   dE  8.713   midcap100/4 vs nifty50/3

      Best achievable in band against the 24, min over three visions: 10.2452
      for a single colour; 8.712634 once six must also separate from each other.

T = 8.712634 IS THE CEILING, AND IT IS BELOW 10. A fifth universe cannot be
given a readable palette at dE 10. It can be given one at 8.71, whose worst
pair under any vision is 2.7x better than the worst pair already shipping.


4. THE FIVE SHIPPED PAIRS ALREADY BELOW 10, AND THEY ALL CO-APPEAR
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
palette_distance.py on the current four universes, deutan:

    dE  3.26   nifty100/v1    vs nifty100/v3
    dE  4.24   midcap150/v2   vs midcap150/v1
    dE  7.35   nifty100/v4    vs nifty100/bh
    dE  7.55   nifty100/v2    vs nifty100/bh
    dE  7.88   midcap150/v3   vs midcap150/ix

All five are WITHIN-universe, which is why the midcap50 registry comment's
"every pair clears dE2000 >= 10" reads as true -- it was scoped to the
cross-universe pairs that search covered. Nothing in the co-appearance
enumeration distinguishes those cases: all five land on the same image under
`--arm all`.

THE STANDARD BEING ENFORCED ON A NEW UNIVERSE WAS STRICTER THAN THE STANDARD
THE SHIPPED PALETTES MEET, by a factor of three on the worst pair. Recorded
here rather than acted on; changing an existing tuple re-renders published
figures and is its own decision.

5. THE CEILING SERIES -- COST THE NEXT UNIVERSE AGAINST THIS, DO NOT RE-DERIVE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Same method each time: step-4 grid over sRGB, readable band L* 30-72, binary
search to 1e-4, dE2000 against every existing slot AND against the tuple's own
six, minimum taken over normal / deutan / protan.

    universes     existing slots   best single      T (6-tuple)     universe
    already wired  searched against  in-band colour   achievable      added
    -------------  ----------------  --------------  -------------  ---------
         4               24              10.2452       8.712634      midcap100
         5               30               8.5514       7.484141      nifty200
         6               36               7.2555       6.801402      smallcap250

THE BAND IS FILLING, AND THE SINGLE-COLOUR FIGURE IS THE LEADING INDICATOR. It
fell 10.2452 -> 8.5514 while T fell 8.712634 -> 7.484141. The single-colour
number is a CEILING on T -- six colours must also separate from each other, so
T is always lower -- and it is cheap to compute, so it is the number to check
first when costing universe seven.

BOTH REMAIN WELL ABOVE WHAT ALREADY SHIPS. The project's worst pair is 3.26
(nifty100/v1 vs nifty100/v3, deutan), so nifty200's 7.484 is 2.3x it and
midcap100's 8.713 was 2.7x.

THE FOURTH POINT, smallcap250 AGAINST 36 SLOTS, CONFIRMS THE DIRECTION AND NOT
A RATE. Single-colour drops 10.2452 -> 8.5514 -> 7.2555 (-1.69, -1.30) and T
drops 8.712634 -> 7.484141 -> 6.801402 (-1.23, -0.68). BOTH DECREMENTS ARE
SHRINKING, so the series is not a straight line down and a linear projection
would understate what is left. smallcap250's worst pairs: 6.900 normal, 6.980
deutan, 6.801 protan -- still 2.1x the 3.26 already shipping.

NOT EXTRAPOLATED. Three points. Nothing here fits a curve, and the two drops
(1.69 single-colour, 1.23 for T) are not evidence of a constant decrement --
they are three differences, and they are getting smaller. nifty500 is the one
universe left; measure, do not project. Recording the series so the next
hand has the measured points rather than re-deriving them, which is the whole
reason this section exists.
