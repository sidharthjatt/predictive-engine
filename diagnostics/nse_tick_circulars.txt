Revision in tick size of securities in CM segment - Update

of securities in Capital Market (CM) segment.  As per the circular, all securities (excluding Exchange Traded
Funds) available in “EQ”, “T0”, “BE”,”BZ”,”BO”, “RL”, “AF” series (and their corresponding “BL” series as
applicable) with security price below Rs 250 will have tick size of Re. 0.01.
