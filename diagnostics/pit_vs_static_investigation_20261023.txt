Why pit beats static on nifty500 and smallcap250 v3/v4 (W1, tax off). Read-only, 2026-10-23.
Inputs: the W1 tax-off run folders named in each section; data/raw membership and price files.
Nothing was rerun and no code was changed. Sections 1-3 answer the three questions,
section 2 for nifty500 static v1 is question 4, sections 5-6 measure where the gap comes from.

HOW THE NUMBERS ARE MADE
- Listed membership: survivorship.load, the `symbols` column of each row with RENAMES, the
  row in force on a date being the latest effective_date on or before it; this is exactly
  what member_mask uses.
- Actual entry: rebuilt from the supplier's events. The earliest row's `symbols`, then each
  later row adds its `inclusions` and removes its `exclusions` (RENAMES applied).
- "Treated as a member before it entered": a (session, symbol) pair in the listed set but not
  in the event set, where the symbol has an inclusion on a later date.
- P&L by symbol: the run's daily_trades (sells net of charges minus buys with charges) plus
  the value held on the last day. It sums to the run's equity change exactly.
- Holding periods: from the fills, a period opens when the position goes from 0 to positive
  and closes when it returns to 0.
- One-day moves: the run's own daily_holdings close on consecutive sessions held.

=== CONCLUSION
Neither membership look-ahead nor bad data explains the pit outperformance.

- Look-ahead by the supplier's own events: 1 nifty500 symbol (GMRAIRPORT, 125 member-sessions)
  and 4 smallcap250 symbols (1,486 member-sessions) are listed before their inclusion date.
  No holding in nifty500 pit v1, v3 or smallcap250 pit v3 falls in those sessions: Rs 0 of P&L.
- The `symbols` column and the inclusion/exclusion columns disagree on thousands of
  (row, symbol) pairs in both directions (nifty500: 1,248 listed with no event, 4,784 entered
  by events but not listed; smallcap250: 708 and 3,524), so neither column is a reliable
  record of entry dates. Holdings in names listed without an inclusion event earn 16.2% and
  4.5% of nifty500 pit v1 and v3 P&L, and 11.8% and 13.2% of smallcap250 pit v1 and v3. Even
  if every one of them were look-ahead and removed, nifty500 pit v1 would keep Rs 23.0m of its
  Rs 27.5m P&L against static's Rs 13.6m.
- Data: P&L earned on days where adj_close and close returns disagree is -1.4% to +0.2% of
  P&L on reverting excursions and -1.0% to +1.7% on all disagreement days, in pit and static
  alike. These are the vendor excursions ratio_clean used to repair; they are back in the
  prices since its removal, and their effect is small and not pit-specific.
- Names outside the static universe earn 7.4% to 7.6% of nifty500 pit P&L, 1.8% of
  smallcap250 pit v1 and 17.0% of v3.
- 17 of nifty500 pit v1's top 20 contributors are in the static universe; 15 of them were
  also traded by static v1, and all 15 earned more in pit (TATAINVEST Rs 2.50m against
  Rs 0.75m, ABREL Rs 1.73m against Rs 0.12m; table 7). The candidate set
  is about 10% wider in pit (nifty500 on 2019-01-01: 397 listed members with a price row
  against 358 of today's members), which changes the z-score peer group, the equal-weight
  market behind beta_60 and idio_vol_60, breadth and the training rows. A concentrated top-8
  book then takes a different path through largely the same names, and the difference
  compounds. The sign of the gap varies by universe (nifty100 pit is 4.8 to 11.3 points
  below static), which fits a path difference rather than a systematic bias.

Not measured: how much a change of candidate set alone moves these figures. Options, none
started:
  (a) check the 13 nifty500 and 16 smallcap250 names listed without an inclusion event
      against NSE's published reconstitution announcements;
  (b) a pit sensitivity run whose membership is the event-built list instead of the
      `symbols` column (needs a run option);
  (c) measure the path spread directly: the existing noise harness on pit and static, or the
      static run on a universe narrowed to the names pit could hold.
