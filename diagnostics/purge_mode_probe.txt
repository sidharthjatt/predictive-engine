====================================================================================================
 WHICH PURGE BUILT THE SHIPPED SCORE PANELS -- DIFFERENTIAL PROBE
====================================================================================================

  A DIFFERENTIAL TEST BETWEEN TWO CANDIDATES. NOT AN IDENTITY REPRODUCTION.
  Each month is refit TWICE from the same on-disk raw panel, once per
  purge_mode, and compared against the shipped score panel. The question is
  which candidate is CLOSER, never whether either one reproduces.

  PURGE = 32 calendar days (legacy)   HORIZON = 20 trading rows   PURGE_EMBARGO = 2 trading rows
  seeds [7, 42, 99, 1, 2, 3, 11, 22, 33, 101]

  DETERMINATION: THE SHIPPED PANELS WERE BUILT WITH purge_mode="trading".
  Unanimous on both primary statistics, the controls confirm the probe has
  no branch preference, and the size-increasing months exclude the
  training-set-size confound.

  THE RESIDUAL, AND WHY THERE IS ONE.
  NEITHER candidate reproduces the shipped panel. The best fit still leaves
  a non-zero mean absolute difference and a four-figure rank disagreement in
  every month, control months included. The cause is known and is not the
  purge: build_scores_*.py score the IN-MEMORY panel and write
  raw_panel_*_cache.csv as a by-product, and the two differ by one unit in
  the last place. This probe can only read the CSV. The control months
  measure that floor directly:
    n100  mean abs floor 2.052e-03 .. 2.318e-03
    mid   mean abs floor 2.077e-03 .. 2.077e-03
  See KNOWN_ISSUES.md, "The headline is not reproducible from the artefacts
  on disk to better than about a point".

  WHAT THIS RUN DID NOT ESTABLISH:
    - that the shipped panels reproduce exactly from the artefacts on disk;
      they do not, and the one-ULP gap above is the reason.
    - anything about whether the trading purge is CORRECT. That is
      diagnostics/leakage_check2_purge.txt. This asks only which one ran.
