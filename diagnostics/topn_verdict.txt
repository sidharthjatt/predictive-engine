  THIS IS NOT A CLAIM THAT TOP_N=12 IS BETTER.
    Its Sharpe is lower in every period,
    and its turnover is higher -- n100 +40.8%, mid +48.1%.
    NO DRAWDOWN CRITERION WAS PRE-REGISTERED, so nothing here is a result
    about drawdown. It is an ungated observation, recorded because the rule
    cannot see it.

    NO DRAWDOWN CRITERION IS ADDED NOW. That would be fitting the rule to
    the data, which is what documenting the Sharpe-only asymmetry rather
    than fixing it was meant to avoid. It stays ungated and recorded.

  WHAT THIS MEANS, AND ITS CEILING
    TOP_N=8 is not beaten by TOP_N=12 on either live
    universe, full period or either half, under entry 10's own criterion,
    measured on the shipping engine.
    IT DOES NOT RE-EARN entry 10's acceptance. The record does not state
    what BUFFER the original 12-arm ran at, so this cannot be shown to be
    entry 10's contrast. NOT CONTRADICTED is the ceiling in every outcome.
    IT DOES NOT MEAN 8 is optimal. Two arms cannot say that.

  NOT GATED, REPORTED ONLY: MaxDD, CAGR, volatility, turnover, mean names
  held and cash-short skips. Entry 10 gated on Sharpe alone and this
  inherits that. A challenger matching Sharpe while halving drawdown would
  not be detected by this rule.

  THIS COUNTS AS A TRIAL and is entered in experiments/EXPERIMENTS.md.
  Calling it a validation constrains what may be DONE with the result --
  no promotion, no second value, no re-run at a changed criterion -- and
  that is the whole of the difference.
