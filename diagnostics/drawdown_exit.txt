  grid 15 cells per universe + control   arm v2   REBAL=20   both live universes

  WHAT CHANGED FROM REVISION 2. THE PEAK IS NOW RESET to the portfolio
  value at re-entry. Revisions 1 and 2 failed from ONE cause -- the peak was
  never reset, so after the March 2020 breach the rule was permanently in
  breach: rev 1 could never re-enter (entry 33), rev 2 re-entered while
  still in breach and oscillated for six years (entry 34).

  WHAT THE RESET GIVES UP, STATED BEFORE THE NUMBERS. Drawdown is no longer
  measured from the strategy's ALL-TIME high. A book that falls THRESHOLD,
  re-enters, and falls THRESHOLD again has lost far more than THRESHOLD from
  its true peak while the rule sees two ordinary breaches: at 15%, two
  resets reach a 27.75% true drawdown, three reach 38.6%. THE RULE NOW
  LIMITS THE LOSS PER EPISODE, NOT THE LOSS OVERALL. It also breaks the
  coherence argued for in spec section 2: reported MaxDD is still all-time,
  the trigger is not, and no comparison between them is drawn here.

  ====================================================================================================================
  THE GOVERNING CONSTRAINT IS UNCHANGED. The window contains ONE severe
  drawdown (March 2020). REPEATED FIRINGS WITHIN THAT ONE EPISODE ARE NOT
  REPEATED TESTS OF THE RULE. Whatever this grid shows, it is readings of
  one event.

  NO ACCEPT RULE. NOTHING CAN BE PROMOTED. MaxDD has NO measured noise floor
  on any arm and entry 28 could not distinguish it from a random null, so the
  rule's PRIMARY OBJECTIVE REMAINS UNJUDGEABLE. Only CAGR cost is inferable:
  measured floors n100 0.97, mid 1.39.
