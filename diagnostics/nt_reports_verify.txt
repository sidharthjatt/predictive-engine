 ORDER STATUS -- what orders.csv can and cannot show
====================================================================================================

  orders.csv comes from eng.trader.generate_order_FILLS_report() (nt_run.py:119).
  That report is built from fill events, so every row it can contain is FILLED by
  construction. 'all FILLED' is therefore partly definitional and is NOT by itself
  evidence that nothing was denied. The independent evidence is below: the strategy
  logs a warning on every denial via on_order_denied (nt_strategy.py:202), and the
  submitted-order count is compared against the filled count.
