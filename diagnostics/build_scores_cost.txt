BUILD_SCORES COST -- the scaling, and a prediction recorded before it is tested
===============================================================================
Written 2026-09-19, after nifty200 and BEFORE smallcap250 is run.

MEASURED POINTS. The [2/2] monthly-scoring stage of build_scores.py, the slow
part of a universe's first run, on this machine, 10-seed ensemble:

    universe      constituents   scoring
    midcap100          98         12.1 min
    nifty200          197         26.5 min

A CORRECTION TO COMMIT 615e257, WHICH GOT THE CAUSE BACKWARDS. nifty200 was
estimated at 24.4 min by scaling midcap100's 12.1 by the 2.01x symbol count,
and came in at 26.5 -- 9% over. That commit said "the shortfall is in the fixed
cost the linear model ignored".

THAT IS BACKWARDS. A positive fixed cost makes scaling SUB-linear: the overhead
is amortised over more symbols, so doubling the symbols less than doubles the
time. nifty200 came in ABOVE proportional, which is the opposite. The cause is
mild SUPERLINEARITY -- cost per symbol rises with n -- not an intercept.

    two-point linear   0.14545 min/symbol, intercept -2.15 min
    power law          cost ~ n^1.123

THE NEGATIVE INTERCEPT IS THE SIGNATURE, NOT A REAL COST. Nothing takes minus
two minutes. A linear fit forced through two superlinear points tilts its
intercept below zero to compensate, and reading that as "fixed overhead" is how
the wrong explanation was reached the first time.

THE PREDICTION, RECORDED BEFORE THE RUN SO IT IS A TEST AND NOT A FIT:

    smallcap250   249 constituents
        linear  33.9 min       power  34.3 min       total run ~38 min

    nifty500      495 constituents
        linear  69.8 min       power  74.6 min

BOTH FITS AGREE AT smallcap250 AND DIVERGE AT nifty500 -- 70 against 75, a 7%
spread. So smallcap250 is the THIRD POINT THAT PICKS BETWEEN THEM, and it can
only do that if the prediction is written down first. Two points cannot
distinguish a line from a power law; three can.

WHAT WOULD FALSIFY THE SUPERLINEAR READING: smallcap250 coming in materially
BELOW 33.9 min. That is what a real fixed cost would look like, and it would
mean the two-point fit was noise rather than curvature.

===============================================================================
THE PREDICTION WAS TESTED AND FAILED. 2026-09-19, after smallcap250.
===============================================================================

    predicted   34.3 min scoring (power law), 33.9 (linear)
    ACTUAL      23.5 min

THE FALSIFIER NAMED ABOVE FIRED. This file said: "what would falsify the
superlinear reading: smallcap250 coming in materially BELOW 33.9 min." It came
in 31% below. The superlinear reading is dead, and so is the fixed-cost reading
that preceded it.

BOTH EXPLANATIONS WERE WRONG BECAUSE BOTH USED THE WRONG VARIABLE. smallcap250
has 248 symbols against nifty200's 197 -- 26% MORE -- and scored FASTER, 23.5
against 26.5 min. No model in the symbol count can produce that. Cost is not a
function of symbol count at all.

    universe      symbols   panel rows   scoring   rows/min
    midcap100          98      383,486   12.1 min    31,693
    nifty200          197      854,197   26.5 min    32,234
    smallcap250       248      798,981   23.5 min    33,999

IT IS THE ROWS, AND IT IS CLOSE TO LINEAR IN THEM. Fitting the first two points
on rows gives 30.592 min per million rows with an intercept of +0.37 min -- a
SMALL POSITIVE intercept, which is what a real fixed cost looks like -- and
predicts smallcap250 at 24.8 against an actual 23.5, a 5% over-prediction
rather than 31%.

WHY SYMBOLS AND ROWS COME APART, AND IT IS THE SAME FACT AS EVERYTHING ELSE ON
THIS UNIVERSE. A panel's rows are symbols x sessions each symbol actually
trades. smallcap250 has 90 of 248 names that did not exist at BT_START_DATE --
36.3%, the highest of the seven -- so a third of its symbols contribute a
fraction of a full history. More names, fewer rows. The late-lister rate is why
the symbol-count model broke here, exactly as it is why the calendar guard
fired here.

THE STANDING ESTIMATE FOR nifty500 IS THEREFORE WITHDRAWN AND REPLACED. The
70-75 min figure was computed from symbol count and is not evidence of
anything. nifty500's row count is not yet measured; count its panel rows first
and apply 30.6 min per million, and DO NOT scale its 495 names against
smallcap250's 248.

WHAT THIS RECORD IS NOW: three measured points, one falsified hypothesis, one
surviving one. The surviving model has been fitted on two points and tested on
a third at 5%. It has NOT been tested outside 0.38-0.85 million rows.
