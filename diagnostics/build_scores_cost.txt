BUILD_SCORES COST -- the scaling, and a prediction recorded before it is tested
===============================================================================
Written 2026-09-19, after nifty200 and BEFORE smallcap250 is run.

MEASURED POINTS. The [2/2] monthly-scoring stage of build_scores.py, the slow
part of a universe's first run, on this machine, 10-seed ensemble:

    universe      constituents   scoring
    midcap100          98         12.1 min
    nifty200          197         26.5 min

A CORRECTION TO COMMIT 615e257, WHICH GOT THE CAUSE BACKWARDS. nifty200 was
estimated at 24.4 min by scaling midcap100's 12.1 by the 2.01x symbol count,
and came in at 26.5 -- 9% over. That commit said "the shortfall is in the fixed
cost the linear model ignored".

THAT IS BACKWARDS. A positive fixed cost makes scaling SUB-linear: the overhead
is amortised over more symbols, so doubling the symbols less than doubles the
time. nifty200 came in ABOVE proportional, which is the opposite. The cause is
mild SUPERLINEARITY -- cost per symbol rises with n -- not an intercept.

    two-point linear   0.14545 min/symbol, intercept -2.15 min
    power law          cost ~ n^1.123

THE NEGATIVE INTERCEPT IS THE SIGNATURE, NOT A REAL COST. Nothing takes minus
two minutes. A linear fit forced through two superlinear points tilts its
intercept below zero to compensate, and reading that as "fixed overhead" is how
the wrong explanation was reached the first time.

THE PREDICTION, RECORDED BEFORE THE RUN SO IT IS A TEST AND NOT A FIT:

    smallcap250   249 constituents
        linear  33.9 min       power  34.3 min       total run ~38 min

    nifty500      495 constituents
        linear  69.8 min       power  74.6 min

BOTH FITS AGREE AT smallcap250 AND DIVERGE AT nifty500 -- 70 against 75, a 7%
spread. So smallcap250 is the THIRD POINT THAT PICKS BETWEEN THEM, and it can
only do that if the prediction is written down first. Two points cannot
distinguish a line from a power law; three can.

WHAT WOULD FALSIFY THE SUPERLINEAR READING: smallcap250 coming in materially
BELOW 33.9 min. That is what a real fixed cost would look like, and it would
mean the two-point fit was noise rather than curvature.
