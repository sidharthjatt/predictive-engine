  4 of 4 gated criteria hold.

  THE RETURN IS NOT REPRODUCED BY RANDOM SELECTION.
  Random scores, with every mechanic held identical, do not match the
  strategy's return on either gated arm on either universe.

  THE CEILING, AND IT IS LOW. This does NOT validate the strategy:
    1. IT DOES NOT CORRECT FOR THE TRIAL DENOMINATOR. This configuration
       was chosen after at least 26 recorded trials on this same data.
       A small p here is NOT the probability that the strategy is noise.
    2. IT DOES NOT ADDRESS SURVIVORSHIP. THE NULL CARRIES THE SAME BIAS --
       every shuffled portfolio is drawn from the same backfilled index
       membership. This test passes just as easily on an inflated universe.
    3. IT DOES NOT MAKE THE RETURN TRADABLE. Synthetic fills, no market
       impact, no capital gains tax.
    4. IT DOES NOT TEST BREADTH. Exposure is invariant under the shuffle.

  THIS COUNTS AS A TRIAL and is entered in experiments/EXPERIMENTS.md
  whichever way it fell.
